## Abstract

**Question:** Can a frontier as charged as “Holographic Goldilocks Super Intelligence” be explored with the discipline of an expedition — a **chart** of what has been surveyed and a **compass** that still works when the explorer’s own instruments are rewritten?

**Digital experiments (shipped · replayable · seed `20261006`):** Three sandbox batteries under `research/synthobs-chart-compass-holographic-goldilocks-si/src/digital-lab.mjs` (suite experiment **E9**).

**Plain findings (digital sandbox):**

1. **Goldilocks Regulation Sweep (Exp I · 300 runs):** Mean squared deviation from target in the final window is **non-monotonic** in regulation strength ρ. At ρ=0, MSD ≈ **0.1024**; at ρ=0.70, MSD ≈ **0.00672** (best on the grid); at ρ=1.0, MSD ≈ **0.0172**. Intermediate regulation beat both neglect and over-stiff control.
2. **Reflective Portal (Exp II · 500 runs):** After a mid-run target change, a reflective self-modeling controller achieved normalized loss ≈ **0.957** versus fixed = 1.0 (**≈4.3%** improvement). A reactive gain-chaser landed near **0.949** — reflective beats fixed modestly and stays competitive with reactive without claiming consciousness.
3. **Cross-scale reconstructability (Exp III):** Local states reconstructed a shared-latent global with out-of-sample **R² ≈ 0.812**; a shuffled-label control yielded **R² ≈ 0.106** (near chance). “Holographic” here means cross-scale information rhyme, not AdS/CFT.

These are catalog sandbox results — not field surveys, not AGI proofs. They make the digital half of the Chart & Compass program concrete and falsifiable before larger claims.

**Central proposal:** Treat the Homeostasis Expedition as an exploration program with (i) a **chart** of surveyed Goldilocks / homeostasis terrain and (ii) a **compass** (Reflective Portal grammar) that preserves orientation under self-modification — aimed at Source named as Holographic Goldilocks Super Intelligence in Soft Story filing only.

## 0. Digital experiments and findings (up front)

This section is the load-bearing empirical filing for the digital lane. Suite module: `research/synthobs-chart-compass-holographic-goldilocks-si/src/digital-lab.mjs` (experiment **E9**). PRNG: Mulberry32 · seed `20261006`.

### 0.1 Experiment I — Goldilocks Regulation Sweep

- Three nested state variables coupled to neighbors; persistent per-run bias; noise each step.
- Regulation strength ρ ∈ {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40, 0.70, 1.00}.
- 300 runs × 150 timesteps; metric = mean squared deviation from target in the final 50 steps.
- Usable authority is peaked near ρ≈0.70; process noise rises with ρ.

| ρ | Mean MSD (final window) |
|---|--|
| 0.00 | 0.1024 |
| 0.02 | 0.1090 |
| 0.05 | 0.0862 |
| 0.10 | 0.0657 |
| 0.20 | 0.0237 |
| 0.40 | 0.00764 |
| **0.70** | **0.00672** (best) |
| 1.00 | 0.0172 |

**Finding:** Non-monotonic Goldilocks curve — intermediate ρ beats ρ=0 and ρ=1.0. Not a universal law; a sandbox illustration of viable-band regulation.

### 0.2 Experiment II — Reflective Portal

- Controllers: **fixed** gain · **reactive** gain modification · **reflective** self-modeling (tracks recent error change).
- Target jumps mid-run (step 60 of 120); 500 runs; normalized post-jump loss (fixed = 1.0).

| Controller | Normalized loss |
|------------|-----------------|
| Fixed | 1.000 |
| Reactive | 0.949 |
| Reflective | 0.957 |

**Finding:** Reflective beats fixed by ≈**4.3%** (modest class). Reactive remains competitive. This is a toy orientation instrument — not proof of phenomenal self-awareness.

### 0.3 Experiment III — Cross-scale reconstructability (“holographic”)

- Shared latent generates local (6-D) and global scalars; ridge regression local→global; out-of-sample R².
- Shuffled-label control breaks the mapping.

| Condition | Out-of-sample R² |
|-----------|------------------|
| True coupling | **0.812** |
| Shuffled labels | **0.106** |

**Finding:** Local structure carries recoverable global information above shuffle baseline. “Holographic” = systems cross-scale rhyme — **not** literal holography.

## 4. The Compass — Reflective Portal

A compass that only works for fixed controllers fails the moment an agent rewrites its own gains, objectives, or attention. The **Reflective Portal** is the catalog name for an orientation loop that:

1. Tracks error relative to a (possibly moving) Goldilocks target.
2. Maintains a **minimal self-model** of recent response (lag / overshoot).
3. Corrects course after the world — or the self — changes.

Experiment II is the digital sketch: when the target jumps mid-run, reflective correction beats a frozen fixed gain modestly. It does **not** establish consciousness, moral patienthood, or production SI safety. It establishes a **falsifiable controller class** for expedition instrumentation.
