## 4. Pre-registered ENGINE_SHELF gate

All must pass on confirmatory held-out:

1. Best dynamic mean NRMSE $\le$ best fixed $\times (1-0.15)$  
2. Same direction on train and hold  
3. Complexity-adjusted score $\ge 0.05$ where score $= \mathrm{improvement\_pct}/\log(1+\mathrm{params}+\mathrm{mean\_switches})$  
4. Paired bootstrap 95% CI on $\Delta=\mathrm{best\_fixed}-\mathrm{best\_dynamic}$ excludes 0 favoring dynamic  
5. Advantage does not require $\Phi$-only affordance  

**On fail:** do **not** add to `ENGINE_SHELF`; ship as application companion only.

## 7. Findings at a glance (live run)

**One-line verdict:** **Nothing to crown** for engine inclusion — confirmatory held-out does not show a significant continuous or discrete win over best fixed; `ENGINE_SHELF` **not** recommended.

| Question | Finding |
|----------|---------|
| Suite protocol integrity? | **Yes** — 4/4 experiments pass as protocol |
| Better constant on confirmatory holdout? | Named arm means edged by **$e$** (~1.106); per-cell best-of-four mean ≈ **1.088**. Not a universal adoption claim — fixture-local. |
| Better combination / continuous policy? | **No.** Best dynamic on hold was **adaptive discrete** (~1.096) — still **worse** than per-cell best fixed (~1.088). Continuous gradient ~1.098; continuous proportional ~1.120; random walk ~1.121. |

| Reinterpret V5/D? | **No** — baselines intact |
