ERFT found **nothing to crown**: no single nest constant, and no dynamic multi-constant controller, beat a simple fixed policy on the locked fixtures. A fourth question sits beside that baseline: maybe recursive stability does not come from one magic number, and maybe it also does not come from switching constants alone — maybe it comes from a **unified three-part architecture** in which $e$ supplies continuous change, $\varphi$ supplies proportional coherence, and primes supply discrete containment.

This expedition implements that architecture as **one system**, not three separate experiments:

$$
C_n \xrightarrow{e\text{-transformation}} T_n \xrightarrow{\varphi\text{-structuring}} S_n \xrightarrow{p_n\text{-containment}} C_{n+1}
$$

The target is **evolution without dissolution**: $D>0$, $B\approx 0$ (or low), $E>0$ — nonzero drift, minimal bleed, useful evolution. Matched controls are ordinary recursion (Control A) and a randomized matched-DOF architecture (Control B). A pre-registered **significance gate** decides whether the result is strong enough for Infinite Octaves `ENGINE_SHELF` inclusion; exploratory publication proceeds either way.

B_n \text{ low}
\quad\text{and}\quad

Objective: **maximize useful evolution subject to bounded drift and minimal bleed.**
