## Abstract

ERFT found **nothing to crown**: no single nest constant, and no dynamic multi-constant controller, beat a simple fixed policy on the locked fixtures. A fourth question sits beside that baseline: maybe recursive stability does not come from one magic number, and maybe it also does not come from switching constants alone — maybe it comes from a **unified three-part architecture** in which $e$ supplies continuous change, $\varphi$ supplies proportional coherence, and primes supply discrete containment.

This expedition implements that architecture as **one system**, not three separate experiments:

$$
C_n \xrightarrow{e\text{-transformation}} T_n \xrightarrow{\varphi\text{-structuring}} S_n \xrightarrow{p_n\text{-containment}} C_{n+1}
$$

The target is **evolution without dissolution**: $D>0$, $B\approx 0$ (or low), $E>0$ — nonzero drift, minimal bleed, useful evolution. Matched controls are ordinary recursion (Control A) and a randomized matched-DOF architecture (Control B). A pre-registered **significance gate** decides whether the result is strong enough for Infinite Octaves `ENGINE_SHELF` inclusion; exploratory publication proceeds either way.

## 1. Motivation from ERFT

ERFT-V5 refused a numerical $\varphi$ crown under constant-neutral recursion. ERFT-D refused a rescue via dynamic switching among fixed constants. Those results stay locked.

The remaining exploratory move is compositional: treat $e$, $\varphi$, and primes as **complementary operators inside one recursive step**, and ask whether the combination produces a Goldilocks regime that neither stagnates ($D\to 0$) nor dissolves ($D\to\infty$ with high bleed).

## 2. Core hypothesis

Explore the conceptual division of roles (hypotheses only — not assigned until data say so):

$$
e \rightarrow \text{continuous transformation},\quad
\varphi \rightarrow \text{recursive proportional coherence},\quad
p \rightarrow \text{discrete containment}
$$

$$
\text{Recursive Homeostasis} \;=\; e\text{-dynamics} + \varphi\text{-structure} + \text{prime containment}
$$

Central claim is **not** “eliminate drift.” Zero drift may be stagnation; unbounded drift may be loss of identity. The proposed Goldilocks regime is:

$$
D_{\mathrm{low}} < D_n < D_{\mathrm{collapse}}
\quad\text{with}\quad
B_n \text{ low}
\quad\text{and}\quad
E_n > 0
$$

Objective: **maximize useful evolution subject to bounded drift and minimal bleed.**

## 3. Unified architecture

Each generation applies all three operations in order. Exact numeric forms are varied under controlled formulations (sequential $e$ then $\varphi$; $e^{k\varphi}$; $\varphi e^{k}$; magnitude-$e$ / structure-$\varphi$). This is **one experimental system**. Ablations (drop $e$, drop $\varphi$, drop prime) are secondary diagnostics; they do not redefine the primary comparison.
