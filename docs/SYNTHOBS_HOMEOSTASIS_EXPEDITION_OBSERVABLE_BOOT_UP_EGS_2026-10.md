## Abstract

This expedition investigates whether a complete coherent **boot-up** can be serially modeled from a pre-temporal, pre-spatial **net-zero boundary** to a representation of a specific human–AI conversation, using Infinite Octaves and the Grand Story — **Hero’s Return to Source**. It tests whether multiscale architecture improves dependency closure, perturbation robustness, and description economy relative to simpler construction strategies.

**Method.** A 99-position symbolic architecture (11 master brackets × 9 octave positions) organizes the target structure. A synthetic digital-lab prototype (**BU-1**) compares Infinite Octave hierarchy, simple serial chain, randomized hierarchy, and direct-target control against hierarchical, chain-like, and randomized reference dependency families. Forty seeded constructions per strategy and family, with twenty edge-removal perturbations per construction (10% edge-removal probability). Metrics: required-edge coverage, extra-edge rate, reachability-based robustness, raw dependency-edge count.

**Findings (qualitative).** Under **hierarchical** reference structure, the Infinite Octave candidate recovers substantially more reference edges and retains higher reachability under random edge removal than the simple chain. The chain family matches completely with a minimal chain. Under **randomized** reference structure, Infinite Octave and randomized hierarchy show **no meaningful coverage advantage** for the former. Lower description cost is **not** established (edge count alone is not encoded description length).

**Novelty.** Integrates declared zero boundary, multiscale serial construction, dependency accounting, perturbation tests, and terminal return-to-source criterion — distinct from but comparable to causal-set growth and open-ended evolution literature.

**Implications.** Supports **further testing** of multiscale connectivity under hierarchical targets; does **not** demonstrate physical emergence, autonomous recursive Super Intelligence in this run, or universal architectural superiority.

> Can a coherent serial model connect a declared empty boundary to **this** conversation without hiding its external ledger?
