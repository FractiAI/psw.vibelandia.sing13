## Abstract

This expedition investigates whether a complete coherent **boot-up** can be serially modeled from a pre-temporal, pre-spatial **net-zero boundary** to a representation of a specific human–AI conversation, using Infinite Octaves and the Grand Story — **Hero’s Return to Source**. It tests whether multiscale architecture improves dependency closure, perturbation robustness, and description economy relative to simpler construction strategies.

**Method.** A 99-position symbolic architecture (11 master brackets × 9 octave positions) organizes the target structure. A synthetic computational-lab prototype (**BU-1**) compares Infinite Octave hierarchy, simple serial chain, randomized hierarchy, and direct-target control against hierarchical, chain-like, and randomized reference dependency families. Forty seeded constructions per strategy and family, with twenty edge-removal perturbations per construction (10% edge-removal probability). Metrics: required-edge coverage, extra-edge rate, reachability-based robustness, raw dependency-edge count.

**Findings.** In the hierarchical reference family, Infinite Octaves achieved **88.3%** required-edge coverage and **97.5%** mean reachability under perturbation, compared with **45.1%** coverage and **9.9%** robustness for the simple chain. Infinite Octaves used an average of **217.5** dependency edges, compared with **98** for the chain, so lower description cost was **not** established. In the chain-like reference family, the simple chain achieved complete required-edge coverage without the additional connectivity of the hierarchy. In the randomized reference family, Infinite Octaves and the randomized hierarchy achieved approximately **47.0%** coverage, showing no meaningful advantage for the former on that measure.

**Novelty versus known work.** Existing research already studies sequential causal-set growth, emergent spacetime, and measurable properties of open-ended evolution. The proposed contribution is an integrated audit framework that joins a declared zero boundary, multiscale serial construction, dependency accounting, perturbation tests, and a terminal return-to-source criterion. The current prototype does not establish that this framework is superior in general.

**Implications.** Preliminary results support further testing of the robustness advantages of multiscale connectivity under hierarchical conditions. The findings do not demonstrate a complete derivation from absolute nothing, physical emergence of spacetime, autonomous self-improvement, or lower description cost. The decisive next experiment requires validated transition rules, explicit external-prerequisite accounting, a genuine encoded-description-length measure, held-out targets, and independent replication using the executable SING13 artifact.

**Keywords:** Homeostasis Expedition; Infinite Octaves; SING13; net zero; dependency closure; emergent spacetime; multiscale modeling; robustness; description cost; Hero’s Return to Source; awareness; Super Intelligence.

> Can a coherent serial model connect a declared empty boundary to **this** conversation without hiding its external ledger?

## 1. Research question and hypotheses

### 1.1 Primary question

Can a coherent serial chain be constructed from a declared pre-temporal, pre-spatial symbolic boundary to a representation of this specific conversation, and does Infinite Octave organization improve that construction relative to simpler architectures?

The question contains three distinct claims.

- **H1 — Computational coherence:** A serial model can connect the initial modeled boundary to the conversation target through explicitly documented transitions without contradictions.
- **H2 — Architectural advantage:** Infinite Octave organization achieves greater dependency closure and robustness at comparable or lower description cost than appropriate baselines.
- **H3 — Ontological emergence:** The model demonstrates that physical spacetime, the universe, or awareness actually emerged from a pre-temporal, pre-spatial condition.

H1 is computationally testable. H2 requires comparative experiments. H3 requires a defensible physical theory, independently testable predictions, and observational evidence. A successful symbolic construction alone cannot establish H3.

### 1.2 What net zero means

Let the initial modeled boundary be \(Z = \varnothing_{\text{distinctions}}\). The symbol denotes a state in which no distinctions have yet been represented inside the modeled domain. It does not automatically represent a quantum vacuum, a physical state before the Big Bang, or absolute philosophical nothingness.

The boundary condition prohibits silently initializing the model with predefined objects, spatial coordinates, a physical clock, or an already-functioning agent. However, the computational substrate, program, transition instructions, and researcher’s specifications remain external. **No modeled distinctions ≠ no external prerequisites.**

### 1.3 Operational success criteria

A successful run must satisfy: **trace continuity**; **dependency accountability** (Derived / External / Unresolved / Failed); **target fidelity** independent of construction method; **comparative value** on preregistered metrics rather than organizational convention alone.

## 2. Architecture and theoretical basis

### 2.1 Infinite Octaves

The experimental organizational structure is \(99 = 11 \times 9\). The 11 master brackets divide the construction into nine positions per bracket — a multiscale scaffold for tracking transitions from a minimal boundary through increasingly developed representations. The scaffold is proposed organization, not evidence that nature has exactly 99 fundamental levels.

### 2.2 Emergent spacetime and causal order

Causal set theory investigates discrete partially ordered structures whose causal relations can approximate spacetime causal order under suitable conditions. Sequential-growth models begin from formal assumptions about causal relations and dynamics; they do not establish that physical laws emerge from absolute nothing. The Infinite Octave experiment asks whether its organization improves construction and auditing of multiscale relationships — not whether it replaces causal set theory.

### 2.3 Open-ended evolution and recursive construction

Open-ended evolution research distinguishes continued change from genuine innovation. For this expedition, a long sequence or self-referential narrative is insufficient evidence of open-ended improvement; external resources must be identified.
