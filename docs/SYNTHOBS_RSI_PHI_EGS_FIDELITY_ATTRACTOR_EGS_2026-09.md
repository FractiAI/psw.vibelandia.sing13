## 1. What “recursive self-improvement” means here

In this filing, RSI is not limited to speculative AGI takeoff narratives. It names any loop where:

1. a system **observes** its own outputs or structure;
2. **modifies** operators, prompts, agents, catalogs, or policies based on that observation;
3. **re-enters** the loop at a deeper or broader scale;
4. and treats prior improvements as **inputs** to the next improvement.

Examples in this repository’s grammar (catalog, not prophecy):

- Nested Agent Lattice — outer meta-optimizer ↔ inner micro-executors (MCA metabolize → crystallize → animate).
- Catalog shelves that nest: digits × octaves → companions → guest surfaces → player moves that rewrite the map.
- Holographic Homeostasis — PARTS ↔ WHOLE ↔ PARTS continuous regulation (dynamic stability, not stillness).

Linear one-pass tooling can survive without a nesting invariant. RSI cannot — each iteration multiplies small incoherences unless a shared ratio / attractor re-aligns scales.

## 2. Drift and fidelity under recursion

### 2.1 Drift (architectural sense)

**Drift** here means progressive loss of shared meaning across iterations: labels shift, scales desynchronize, local wins contradict global viability, and “improvement” becomes noise accumulation.

Peer language already on the shelf ([EGS Catalog Frontier](./SYNTHOBS_INFINITE_OCTAVE_EGS_CATALOG_FRONTIER_EGS_2026-09.md) Part I):

> Without an invariant nesting grammar, each capability leap can inject unquantifiable drift *inside that paradigm*.

### 2.2 Fidelity (architectural sense)

**Fidelity** means that after $n$ recursive improvements, local states still map coherently to the whole’s Goldilocks region — the viable band ([Goldilocks ≡ Net Zero](./SYNTHOBS_GOLDILOCKS_NET_ZERO_EQUIVALENCE_EGS_2026-09.md)), produced by continuous regulation ([Holographic Homeostasis](./SYNTHOBS_HOLOGRAPHIC_HOMEOSTASIS_EGS_2026-09.md)).

Holographic rhyme files self-correction as phase-drift dampening *talk* toward harmonic zero — metaphor for balance under motion, not measured ECC retirement ([Holographic rhyme](./SYNTHOBS_HOLOGRAPHIC_RHYME_FRACTAL_EGS_2026-09.md)).

### 2.3 Why RSI makes the problem acute

| Regime | Drift risk | Why |
|--------|------------|-----|
| One-shot tool | Low–moderate | Single pass; errors stay local |
| Multi-agent parallel | Moderate | Peer firewall limits mesh contamination |
| **Recursive self-improvement** | **High without invariant** | Each loop writes the next loop’s environment |

RSI is therefore the regime where interest in a nesting constant becomes *rational architecture hunger*, not decorative numerology.
