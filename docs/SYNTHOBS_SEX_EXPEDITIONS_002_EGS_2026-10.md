## Abstract

This expedition investigates how biological foundations, physical senses, internal bodily states, cognitive processes, intuitive judgments, imagination, experience, memory, context, and ongoing regulation may jointly contribute to the emergence and tuning of sensations, urges, emotions, traits, behaviors, orientations, preferences, and biological and behavioral cycles. Its broader research setting is the study of sex as a major recursive branch of Perpetual Recursive Self-Improvement within the Fractal-Holographic Goldilocks Super Intelligent Omniversal Universe. The present paper focuses on the sensory-to-behavioral architecture that can help organize that investigation.

The proposed architecture connects seven functional layers: biological substrate; signal detection and bodily state; integration and significance; sensory, cognitive, intuitive, and imaginative experience; interpretation and regulation; response and action; and learning and longer-term tuning. Five analytical dimensions distinguish biological foundations, experience-layered tuning, biological–experiential interaction, current context, and unresolved attribution. An expanded catalog explicitly includes physical senses, interoception, cognitive sensing, intuition, imagination, mental imagery, emotion, motivation, traits, behavior, attraction and orientation, preferences, learning, cycles, social dynamics, molecular mechanisms, sexual and reproductive phenomena, wellbeing, values, and cross-scale biological portals.

A targeted literature review identifies substantial prior work in systems biology, dynamical systems, predictive processing, active inference, homeostasis and allostasis, control theory, multilevel psychology, imagery, intuition, and metacognition. Consequently, multiscale integration and feedback alone cannot establish the proposed architecture’s novelty. Its candidate contribution is a common operational catalog linking these areas to testable pathways, explicit cross-scale hypotheses, and a measurable multi-objective “Goldilocks” operating region.

A preliminary synthetic simulation compared four model classes and an ablation of cross-scale feedback over a **500-step** artificial time series (seed `20261023`). In the locked pilot run, the **additive-memory** model had the lowest one-step tracking RMSE (**0.1435**), while the recursive multiscale model showed a lower mean absolute step change (**0.0603**) than additive-memory (**0.0862**). Recursive multiscale did not outperform all alternatives; the ablation improved tracking RMSE relative to the full recursive model (**0.1767** vs **0.3615**) while the recursive model remained smoother—illustrating a metric trade-off rather than a universal win. These results are illustrative computational results, not evidence about human biology or proof of architectural superiority.

The next research stage is a reproducible, database-based systematic review followed by technical benchmarking, ablation studies, parameter sensitivity tests, and validation on independent empirical datasets. The expanded catalog also creates a specific research task: to distinguish intuitive judgments based on rapid or implicit processing from imagination, deliberate reasoning, and perceptions that are attributed to sources outside ordinary sensory channels.

## 3. Scope and evidential conventions

Three levels of claim are distinguished:

1. **Established background** — supported by existing research.
2. **Proposed architecture** — organizing structure proposed here.
3. **Unvalidated hypothesis** — requires experiments, comparison, or replication.

The review is a **targeted literature synthesis**, not a completed database-wide systematic review. The synthetic simulation is an artificial feasibility test—not human-subject data.

### 3.1 Necessary distinction: senses, cognitive processes, and imagined experiences

| Category | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Physical senses** | Vision, hearing, smell, taste, touch, balance, etc. |
| **Interoceptive / bodily sensing** | Hunger, heartbeat, breathing, temperature, etc. |
| **Cognitive sensing** | Noticing patterns, contradictions, social shifts—not necessarily a separate sense organ |
| **Intuition** | Rapid judgment with little deliberate reasoning; may reflect learning, heuristics, or bias |
| **Imagination** | Generating mental representations not currently perceived |

These categories can interact. They are not equivalent. Reports of a “sixth sense” or extrasensory perception may be studied as reports; they are not assumed to demonstrate a new sensory channel without controlled, independently replicated evidence.

## 4. The seven-layer sensory-to-behavioral architecture

1. **Biological substrate** — molecules, cells, organs, pathways that make sensing possible.
2. **Signal detection and bodily state** — environmental and internal signals.
3. **Integration and significance** — multisensory integration, attention, salience, working memory.
4. **Sensory, cognitive, intuitive, and imaginative experience** — physical qualities, bodily feelings, cognitive impressions, intuitive judgments, imagined scenarios, affective significance.
5. **Interpretation and regulation** — appraisal, prediction, confidence, inhibition, planning.
6. **Response and action** — approach, withdraw, communicate, refrain, etc. (urge ≠ action).
7. **Learning and longer-term tuning** — updating expectations, habits, preferences, intuition calibration.

**Recursive connections** allow upward and downward information flow. The seven layers are a functional map, not a fixed sequence claim.

## 5. Five dimensions of tuning

| Code | Dimension | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **B** | Biological foundation | Genetic, developmental, molecular, physiological, neural, endocrine, immune contributions |
| **E** | Experience-layered tuning | Learning, memory, conditioning, practice, past outcomes |
| **B×E** | Biological–experiential interaction | Effects depending on interaction of biological state and experience |
| **C** | Current context | Immediate environment, relationships, culture, goals, stressors, resources |
| **U** | Unresolved attribution | Uncertainty about mechanism or relative contribution |

These are not mutually exclusive causes or fixed numerical shares. Strength of an intuition does not establish validity.

### 6.1–6.4 Interpretation rules

- Do not collapse sensation, intuition, imagination, confidence, and accuracy into one variable.
- Do not treat vivid imagery as literal observation.
- Do not treat compelling intuition as proof of a valid external cause.
- Do not assume one causal direction.
- Define measurements before scoring a model; record uncertainty explicitly.

**Cognitive sensing** = functional pattern detection via cognition. **Intuition** = potentially useful but fallible rapid judgment. **Imaginary senses** = imagination-based sensory experience (mental imagery / simulation), not extrasensory proof.

## 7. Molecular foundations and selective biological portals

Water and carbon-based chemistry are central to known terrestrial biology; N, P, S, metals, and ions also matter. Selective receptors, channels, transporters, enzymes, synapses, membranes, and endocrine pathways can be treated as **biological portals**—structures that select, transmit, transform, or restrict passage. “Molecular awareness” here is a **functional metaphor** for sensitivity and state-dependent response—not a claim that molecules have subjective experience.

## 11. Preliminary synthetic simulation: continuity and model comparison

### 11.1 Purpose

Can continuity, tracking error, and recovery following a disturbance be measured in an artificial time-series setting?

### 11.2 Design

- **500** steps; temporal persistence; temporary disturbance steps **250–299**.
- Shared target + observation-noise sequence; seed **`20261023`**.
- Models: feedforward chain; additive memory; feedback controller; recursive multiscale; recursive ablation (slow cross-scale removed).
- Metrics: one-step tracking RMSE; mean absolute step change; recovery-window MAE (steps **300–399** vs lagged target); continuity ratio (|Δ| < 0.5).
- Pipeline: `npm run research:synthobs-sex-expeditions-002`.

### 11.3 Locked pilot results (seed 20261023)

| Model | Tracking RMSE ↓ | Mean \|Δ\| ↓ | Recovery MAE ↓ | Continuity ratio ↑ |
|-------|----------------:|-------------:|---------------:|-------------------:|
| Feedforward chain | 0.6900 | 0.0726 | 0.4089 | 1.000 |
| **Additive memory** | **0.1435** | 0.0862 | **0.0410** | 0.990 |
| Feedback controller | 0.2184 | 0.0647 | 0.1123 | 0.998 |
| Recursive multiscale | 0.3615 | **0.0603** | 0.2815 | 1.000 |
| Recursive ablation | 0.1767 | 0.0729 | 0.0715 | 0.996 |

### 11.4 Interpretation

1. **Additive memory** had the lowest tracking RMSE.
2. **Recursive multiscale** was smoother (lowest mean |Δ|) than additive memory but not best on tracking.
3. **Ablation** improved tracking RMSE vs full recursive (**0.1767** vs **0.3615**) while recursive remained smoother—a **trade-off**, not a universal win.
4. Continuity ratio near ceiling for all models at threshold 0.5—limited discrimination (needs sharper metrics later).

### 11.5 What the pilot establishes

The recursive multiscale idea can be expressed as state-transition equations and compared with simpler baselines. It does **not** establish human sensation/intuition/sexuality accuracy, novelty, or superiority on independent data.
