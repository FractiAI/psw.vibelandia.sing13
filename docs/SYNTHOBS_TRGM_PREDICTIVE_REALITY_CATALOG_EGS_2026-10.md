## Abstract

**Primary question.** Can TRGM #50 be independently reproduced from a clean checkout, and does the wave/node/fq catalog add predictive value on held-out biological regions against matched baselines — while a functional reality catalog stores recipes for retrieve, compare, and recombine?

**Methods.** Node.js suite · reproduce #50 fixtures + runner · held-out split (chr21 train genes vs non-chr21 + chrM held) · matched baselines (shuffle · conventional · sequence-proxy · flat encode · null layout) · channel-assignment contrast · reality catalog schema `trgm-reality-catalog-record/v1`. Seeds `20261025` / `202610251`.

Reality catalog `trgm-51/v1`: **9** records with compare + recombine.

**Limitation.** Computational Soft Story architecture validation — not a claim about molecular mechanisms inside living chromosomes. Biology claims stay on a separate wet-lab validation track.

**Keywords:** TRGM; predictive validation; held-out; baselines; wave; node; fq; reality catalog; Homeostasis Expedition.

## 1. Introduction

TRGM #50 shipped a structured three-symbol cyclic stream × polygon spin × Infinite Octave rhyme tree on folded DNA, with genetic nodes catalogued to wave / node / fq. The next milestone is not a louder metaphor. It is independent predictive validation and a reusable reality catalog:

1. Reproduce #50 from a clean checkout (hashes, provenance, runner, locked metrics).  
2. Test on held-out biological regions — avoid tuning the grammar and scoring it on the same examples.  
3. Compare against strong matched baselines.  
4. Ask whether wave/node/fq assignments add predictive value.  
5. Store each generated structure so it can be retrieved, regenerated, compared, and recombined.  
6. Keep biology claims on their own validation track.

## 4. Methods

- **Reproduce:** `src/reproduce50.mjs` — SHA-256 of pinned fixtures under `research/synthobs-tiny-reality-generation-model/data/molecular/genetic/` · import `runAllExperiments` from #50 · lock seed `20261025`, n_total=24, held viability=1, null=0, catalogEntries=199.  
- **Held-out lab:** `src/heldout-baselines.mjs` — split genes by chrom; score balanced accuracy of feature-kind grammar vs shuffle / conventional / sequence-proxy on held non-gene features.  
- **Architecture:** Φ holographic-rhyme tree vs flat encode coherence; folded DNA viability vs null scatter.  
- **Channel contrast:** polygon-spin clock sync on correct triad stream vs motif-hit rate after symbol permutation.

## 6. Discussion

Independent reproduction closes the “works on my machine” gap for #50. Held-out scoring on non-gene feature kinds avoids Soft Story gene-primary tautology: the transferable grammar is the feature-kind → channel map, not memorized gene labels. Sequence-proxy can match grammar BA when CpG/CDS heuristics align with the same map — that is reported honestly beside shuffle and conventional, not hidden. Architecture baselines reconfirm that recursion and DNA theater matter for coherence and viability. The reality catalog turns one-off generator runs into addressable, recombinable artifacts.

Biology stays on a separate track. A computational DNA-inspired representation is a legitimate research architecture; a claim about molecular mechanisms inside living chromosomes requires independent biological evidence that this suite does not supply.
