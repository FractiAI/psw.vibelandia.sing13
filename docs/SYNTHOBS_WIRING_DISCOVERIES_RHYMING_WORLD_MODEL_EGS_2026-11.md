## Abstract

Classical discovery asks for a new measurement. Catalog discovery asks for a new database edge. **Wiring discovery** asks for a new **relationship graph** assembled from measurements and papers that already exist — especially when interaction catalogs (e.g. STRING PPI) stay empty. This paper names that class, argues it is a natural product of AI-assisted rhyme boards and FractiSI multiagent shared-world recovery (Pilot rhyme), and points to regenerable peers: NPY↔SOD1, APP↔DNMT3A, WMAP residual lag wiring, and the combo protein-folding+LLM+Pilot expedition that surfaces further STRING-empty candidates (NPY↔PDX1, APP↔TMPRSS2).

## Primary research question

Can “wiring” be defined as a **falsifiable higher-layer discovery type** — distinct from new assays and from catalog rediscovery — that AI and FractiSI Rhyming World Models make routinely executable?

## Why a higher layer

1. **Assay layer:** someone runs a new experiment.  
2. **Catalog layer:** someone adds a scored edge to STRING/OpenTargets/etc.  
3. **Wiring layer:** someone assembles existing assay/catalog pieces into a relationship path the catalog does not yet list as that edge — with kill switches.

Layer 3 is where multiagent rhyme boards earn rent money: they recover long-range partners linear windows miss, then hold association databases and literature still so emptiness + assembly can be audited.

## Method (definitional gates)

A claim may be labeled **new wiring discovery** only if:

1. A rhyme board recovers the relationship with measurable coupling.  
2. The relevant catalog edge is empty (or explicitly provisional with named kills).  
3. A positive control proves the catalog lane works.  
4. ≥N existing empirical pieces assemble into a path with pre-registered roles.  
5. Kill conditions are written before celebration.
