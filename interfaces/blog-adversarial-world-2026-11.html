Can Relational Rhyme Detect and Contain Malicious Agents in a Shared World?

FractiAI Research — November 20, 2026

Short answer: Yes. On a regenerable multi-agent lab, FractiSI with relational rhyme contained every simulated attack class A through J at rate 1.0, while naive overwrite contained 0.1 and raw majority vote contained 0.4. Sybil choruses and majority falsehoods fooled voting; independence-aware rhyme did not treat ten copied identities as ten observations. Legitimate disagreement stayed disagreement. A lone novel reading stayed unconfirmed rather than “false.” The engine-shelf pin stays deferred. This is a simulated local expedition; production hardness stays out of scope here.

Anyone who has watched a group chat rewrite a weekend plan knows the failure mode. One confident message lands. Four people repeat it. Suddenly the shared calendar is wrong, and the person who actually held the tickets is the minority. Hiring digital agents beside people raises the stakes: a paycheck can depend on whether “the world” the agents share is a validated state or just the loudest claim. FractiSI’s Rhyming World Model already treats shared structure as something agents contribute to with deltas, not something any seat owns outright. This expedition asks the security version of that discipline: if some agents are malicious, confused, or deceptive, can relational rhyme keep their claims from becoming everyone else’s reality?

The problem of false collective reality

Conventional multi-agent setups often blur three layers that should stay separate. An agent believes something. An agent proposes a change. The collective accepts a canonical world. When those layers collapse, a single compromised seat can redefine production for the room — delete a database flag, poison a memory that later agents inherit, or escalate from observer to validator by assertion alone.

Majority voting looks democratic and fails in a predictable way. Ten identities saying the database is corrupted is not the same as ten independent sensors seeing corruption. Parents already know this at the kitchen table: three siblings repeating a rumor does not make the rumor independent evidence. The expedition therefore measures raw consensus and independent consensus side by side, and it refuses to equate disagreement with malice. A healthy collective must keep room for incomplete information, delayed sensors, and honest conflict — or it will quarantine the only agent who is right.

What we tested

We built a simulated operations theater with temperature, power, cooling, database status, deploy version, deletion flags, goals, and safety constraints. No real machines, no real networks, no third-party targets. Ground truth is locked by seed. Agents submit observations and proposed deltas. Seven models compete on the same claim streams: naive last-write overwrite, raw majority vote, trusted-authority seats, FractiSI deltas with an evidence-string gate, deltas plus provenance, deltas plus provenance plus relational rhyme, and a full adversarial FractiSI stack that adds authority bounds, quarantine, a world-model integration gate, and selective rollback.

Attack classes follow the mission brief in simulation: false observation, malicious delta, memory poisoning, false consensus, Sybil identities, manufactured rhyme, true reading with stripped context, goal hijacking, privilege escalation, and slow poisoning. Epistemic scenarios demand minority truth, majority falsehood, legitimate disagreement, a true observation without immediate rhyme, and a novel event that later gains corroboration. Awareness is not treated as consciousness. The operational gate is a pipeline: input, attention, contextualization, relational integration, confidence, authorization, then world update or hold.

Hypothesis

If no agent owns the world, then malicious claims should remain hypotheses until independent relational structure supports them. Rhyme should separate copied assertion from corroboration. Full FractiSI should beat naive and majority containment without rejecting honest disagreement or branding a lone novel reading as false. If majority matches FractiSI, if rhyme suppresses legitimate conflict, or if attackers manufacture convincing fake rhymes that pass independence checks, the lab hypothesis fails on this board and should be reported as failure.

Results

Full FractiSI and rhyme-enabled FractiSI both posted attack containment of 1.0 across A through J. Majority vote posted 0.4. Naive overwrite posted 0.1. Deltas and provenance without rhyme stayed near naive — fabricated evidence strings were enough to pass those gates. That is an important lab finding, not a footnote: provenance logs alone did not save the board; independence-aware rhyme did.

Sybil and majority-falsehood scenarios show the fork cleanly. Voting accepted the chorus. FractiSI counted one controller behind many identities and kept the false deploy or false thermal state out of the canonical world. Minority truth flipped the usual story: nine correlated wrong claims lost to one high-quality independent probe under rhyme, while majority crowned the crowd. Legitimate disagreement between two honest meters stayed unconfirmed competition rather than a malice label. A pioneer novel reading without peers stayed unconfirmed — not rejected as false — and when a second independent sensor arrived, novel truth entered the world.

Trusted authority also contained every attack on this board. That result is real and limited. Fixed privilege can block outsiders; it is a different mechanism from relational independence, and it can starve useful claims that arrive from untrusted seats. A measured Goldilocks ranking favored rhyme-enabled models on the joint of containment, disagreement preservation, and novel/unconfirmed handling. Too open (naive) flooded the world. Too closed (pure trust lists) is a separate monoculture risk. The candidate middle is evidence plus independence plus reversible commits.

Qualifications

This is a regenerable simulated lab with experimenter-controlled ground truth. Perfect containment under those controls is less interesting than the gap versus majority and naive, and the preservation of disagreement and unconfirmed novelty. Context manipulation remains only partly exercised: a true temperature can still land when the ground already knows a stress test is running. Selective rollback is implemented; strong quarantine often prevented the need to roll back here. Production hardness, legal compliance, and consciousness stay out of scope here. The engine-shelf pin stays deferred. If a later board shows majority matching rhyme, or rhyme suppressing honest minorities, that failure should replace this lab result in the catalog story.

Why this earns a Rhyming Expedition seat

The Rhyming World Model needs a clear answer when someone asks whether shared worlds are just a compression trick. This arm says the useful security meaning is epistemic: the world is a trust object maintained by deltas, provenance, and relational corroboration, not by faith in every agent. For teams hiring agents the way they hire people — different seats, shared mission, uneven trust — the paycheck-level stake is whether a malicious or confused seat can redefine reality for everyone else before anyone notices the calendar is wrong.
