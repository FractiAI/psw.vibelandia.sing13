What “awareness” means in plain English

Awareness, in this house style, means filed indexing. It means the catalog already knows which vault, which octave, which prime-container sketch applies before a child helper invents a private cosmology mid-loop. Mystical glow is someone else’s product. Model personhood is someone else’s theater. Treating humans as “one protein” is a slogan that collapses biology into a joke — the kind of joke that does not help a neighbor who is trying to understand what AI actually costs at the dinner table.

Parents already teach kids a version of awareness: look where you left the shoes before you tear the house apart. Teachers already teach a version: use the index before you reread the whole book. Only one should be the default religion.
