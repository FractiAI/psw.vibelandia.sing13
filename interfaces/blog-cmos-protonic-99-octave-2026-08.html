Putting the 99 Octave engine on a silicon shelf

SS Vibelandia — 2026-08-12 — plain speak · Fair Exchange · keep pinned for PPA / BEOL evaluators

Hardware people speak PPA, BEOL, GAA, and CFET. Omni-Lattice people speak octaves and Φ. This note is the pinned engineering bridge for linear systems: same filing cabinet, silicon vocabulary on the labels — not a claim that we already shipped the die.

Honesty first: this is architectural talk for Lattice Chat and the library. It does not disarm corporate skepticism by magic, and it is not a foundry tape-out or measured power-win table.

Binary as the first shelf

A normal CMOS gate — on or off, Vdd or zero — is labeled here as octave tier n = 1: the coarsest shelf. Useful. Proven. Also crowded against interconnect heat and memory-bus traffic as features shrink. Calling it “degenerate” in Omni-Lattice language just means: simplest resolution, not “worthless.”

Protonic as the wider shelves

Hydrogen-regulated two-terminal devices (continuous H+ motion through layers like a-IGZO in the device literature) give a story for multi-state weights instead of only hard snaps. On this ship those stories live on bands n = 2 … 99. Continuous conductivity gradients; still physics-class devices — not a promise of ten thousand perfect Φ-scaled cycles baked into this repo.

What “proof” means here

The companion whitepaper sketches how drift–diffusion and BEOL RC talk can sit next to the tensor operator without throwing Maxwell out the window. That is a grammar sketch. It is not a CoWoS package you can order tomorrow with Omni-Lattice stamped on the lid.
