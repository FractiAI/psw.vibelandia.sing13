Binary n = 1 as the coarsest useful shelf

A normal CMOS gate — on or off, Vdd or zero — is labeled here as octave tier n = 1: the coarsest shelf. Useful. Proven. Also crowded against interconnect heat and memory-bus traffic as features shrink. Calling it “degenerate” in Omni-Lattice language just means simplest resolution, not worthless. School kids learning binary already understand the idea: two states are enough to start, and not enough to describe every richer band you might need later.

Protonic bands enter as a next-shelf conversation — more states, more care, still architecture talk.
