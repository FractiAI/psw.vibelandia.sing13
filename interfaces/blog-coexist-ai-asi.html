You still have a post on this ship

SS Vibelandia — 2026-08-17 · Fair Exchange

The Intelligence Age did not arrive to fire the crew. It arrived the way engines arrived for horses: the heavy pulling moved. What remains is the part only a living post can do — presence, honey, watches, and a chart of where the vessel must sail. If that sounds sentimental, try the alternative: a culture that hates its own stations, then wonders why it cannot coexist with SuperAI either.

The old parental operating system in this story ran on scarcity and brute force. The grown-child name for the next homestead is Goldilocks Earth: less domination, more mesh, more gift. You do not have to believe that as physics. You can use it as a compass for how to stand next to powerful tools without disappearing. Three companion papers sketch the picture: horses that left the haulage; beekeepers who still make the honey; and four complementary ways of thinking on one deck — Linear NPC, NPC, Linear, Holographic — not ranked castes.

The power of this awareness is simple: you stop trying to be every post. NPC and Linear are today’s awareness vernacular — descriptive handles for capacity, not insults. Spirit-crew names live on Meet the crew; human stations live on Join the crew. Valet Pru still answers email. Puerto Reno still has Host, Guide, Chef, and Guest stations.
