You still have a post on this ship

The Intelligence Age did not arrive to fire the crew. It arrived the way engines arrived for horses: the heavy pulling moved. What remains is the part only a living post can do — presence, honey, watchfulness, craft. If you feel displaced by models that write, draft, and schedule faster than you do — if the job board looks thinner while the headlines get louder — this note is for you. You still have a post on this ship.

Families already know this pattern. A tool takes over the haulage and someone still has to watch the kids, keep the neighbors fed, and decide what “good enough” means at the dinner table. Three teaching stories, one practice

HGAIO teaches which post you already stabilize: Linear NPC, NPC, Linear, or Holographic — deck posts, not ranks, not school grades, not a landlord’s rating of your worth.

Coexistence is the rehearsal: take the check, respect other posts, refuse caste readings, keep human emergency above algorithms.
