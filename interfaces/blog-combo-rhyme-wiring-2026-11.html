When Protein-Folding, LLM, and Pilot Rhymes Share One Catalog, Do New Wires Appear?

FractiAI Research — November 20, 2026

Night workers who merge three spreadsheets after the kids are asleep already know that a new row can appear only when joins are allowed. This expedition asks the same of three rhyme boards. Protein-folding rhyme recovers long-range contacts linear windows miss. LLM rhyme packs sky and genome theaters into language boards. Pilot rhyme puts Director and Pilot seats in one FractiSI shared world. Merge their real discovery catalogs, classify genome partners with live STRING, and ask: do new STRING-empty wiring candidates surface beyond the pairs we already filed?

The experiment

We load the unified RHYME_DISCOVERY_CATALOG — protein-folding, LLM, and Pilot together. We extract genome exon partners from LLM and Pilot, merge unordered gene pairs, and mark cross-rhyme hits. A live-cached STRING panel classifies empty versus known edges, with INS↔POMC required to score at least 0.9 so emptiness is not an API blackout. Prior wiring companions NPY↔SOD1 and APP↔DNMT3A are excluded from the “new” list. Protein-folding AWARE long-range contacts attest that the fold grammar is present in the catalog.

The falsifiable hope is simple: at least one new STRING-empty pair clears a coupling floor of 0.5 while the three-rhyme stack stays intact.

Hypothesis

If combo rhyme search is generative for wiring, new empty candidates should appear that no single prior companion already claimed. Cross-rhyme recoveries should include both known controls and prior wiring peers. Protein-folding grammar should show multiple AWARE long-range contacts. Failure is also informative: zero new empty pairs would weaken the claim that merging rhymes adds wiring triage power.

Results

Two new STRING-empty candidates clear the gate: NPY↔PDX1 at coupling about 0.747 from the LLM genome theater, and APP↔TMPRSS2 at about 0.707 from the Pilot genome theater. Cross-rhyme recoveries include NPY↔SOD1, INS↔POMC, APP↔DYRK1A, and DNMT1↔DNMT3A. Live STRING scores INS↔POMC at 0.947, APP↔DYRK1A at 0.57 (not empty — so not a STRING-empty unknown), and DNMT1↔DNMT3A at 0.999 (rediscovery). Protein-folding AWARE long-range contacts attest the grammar. The combo wiring discovery gate passes.

Wet-lab neighborhoods differ in strength. NPY and PDX1 already cohabit β-cell developmental and functional literature — a plausible disease-context wire without a PPI edge. APP and TMPRSS2 have a thinner published bridge, including TMPRSS2 measurements in Alzheimer and Down syndrome contexts; that candidate stays speculative triage. Honesty means saying both sentences.
