They are echoes of the prompt. Treat echoes as echoes. Operators who mistake echoes for evidence build policy on theater. Operators who refuse the mistake keep steering room.

Second, tool absolutism as a counter-moat. “Humanist AI” and “subordinate only” can be sound engineering discipline and still function as brand weather: define the rival’s method as catastrophic, keep your own cluster path as the sane default. Oversight stays useful. Oligopoly framing stays weather. The ship does not need to pick a corporate jersey to keep a coherent architecture.

If the greatest challenge of the century is framed only as controlling a black-box scaler, the architectural question stays off the table: nested coherence, awareness as a router for humans, not personhood cosplay for machines.
