Yes — Two Candidate Physics Leftovers on WMAP

FractiAI Research — November 17, 2026

Candidate novel physics, named: after we stripped how catalogs are built and smoothed the known microwave continuum, two leftover patterns on the WMAP temperature spectrum — residual lags 11 and 14 — still beat our null tests and sit off the locked acoustic-spacing template. We call them candidate novel physics under this protocol. We do not call them a confirmed new force, particle, or law. Beam, mask, and instrument stories remain open. That is the physics headline. The rest of this note is how a protein-folding lens got us there without pretending the first pass was already physics.

The candidates, in kitchen English

People ask the physics question first, and they should. The answer is not “nothing.” It is “two leftovers that cleared the residual sieve.” Lag eleven and lag fourteen on the residual WMAP spectrum beat shuffled-number nulls after the smooth continuum was removed. Nearby lags that lean toward the known acoustic spacing, and exoplanet mass–radius leftovers after discovery year and distance were taken out, came back labeled as rediscoveries — which is what a careful search should do when the leftover structure is familiar. A sieve that only finds fireworks is broken. A sieve that also rediscovers the familiar is how you trust the leftover that still does not fit. Anyone who has audited a family budget after a surprise bill knows that feeling.

What the first arms actually found

The first three arms were never a physics claim. They showed that a relationship board recovers partners a scroll-through misses on NASA exoplanets, JPL close approaches, OpenNGC deep-sky objects, and the WMAP microwave spectrum — roughly zero percent under the weak reads, full recovery under the relationship board on our long-range tests. Open motif search, not told to hunt filaments or dark matter, kept patterns about how the observable cosmos is recorded: when planets were discovered lines up with what kinds of host stars surveys were watching; how big deep-sky objects look lines up with where they sit and how bright they are listed. Useful for anyone who has cleaned a cross-agency table between shifts. Not a new landlord for the universe — which is exactly why residual search was required.

Why residual search was the missing step

Method that travels is novel for a research program. Catalog fingerprints are moderately novel as observation about selection. Neither upgrades into physics. Physics, under our protocol, means leftovers after known selection and known templates are removed, then tested against nulls. That is C4. Two WMAP residual lags cleared that bar. Seven known-template hits also returned and were labeled rediscovery. We keep the word candidate in the headline on purpose so nobody has to dig past a soft “maybe” to find the claim.

What the residual search returned, line by line

Two WMAP residual lags — eleven and fourteen — beat nulls after continuum removal and sit off the locked acoustic-spacing template: those are the candidates. Seven known-template rediscoveries also returned, including acoustic-leaning lags near seventeen through nineteen and exoplanet mass–radius couplings after year and distance were removed. Parents who have waited for a school report and analysts who have waited for a spreadsheet join know the difference between a leftover that still does not fit and a leftover that matches a category you already know. We name both. We do not promote rediscoveries into fireworks, and we do not demote cleared candidates into “nothing found.”

Why naming candidates still earns a seat

Science that only ships fireworks trains readers to ignore quiet results that pay the rent. Science that only says “we found nothing” when leftovers cleared a protocol trains readers to miss the next useful fight. Teachers, analysts, and researchers stretched between paycheck and curiosity need the same discipline: change the representation, hold the materials still, remove what you already know how catalogs are built, let nulls decide, and say plainly which leftovers are candidates and which are rediscoveries.
