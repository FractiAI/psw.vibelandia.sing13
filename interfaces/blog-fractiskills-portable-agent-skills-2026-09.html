FractiSkills is the opposite move: portable agent skills you can carry between rooms — small enough to travel, sharp enough to keep nesting coherent — so Lattice Chat inherits craft instead of reinventing religion every turn.

The stake is human. Builders burn out when every session starts from zero. Guests get lost when every agent invents private nouns. Captains lose the thread when “skill” means a vibe instead of a pack you can tip for, version, and reuse.

What “portable” means when agents nest

Portable does not mean “copy the universe into the prompt.” It means a skill pack with a job, inputs, outputs, and an honesty posture. A child agent can load it without renegotiating the parent’s worldview. A human can move it from Lattice Chat to a research suite without rewriting the brand.

That is Seed:Edge in practice. Seed is the origin pack. Edge is the experience when it runs. Executive prompts fill the middle without pretending the middle is the whole product. Guests feel hospitality. Operators feel less thrash.

Vibe coding often treats skills as improvisation. FractiSkills treats skills as luggage you can inspect. Luggage can be light. Luggage can be wrong. Luggage can be audited. Improvisation that never becomes luggage stays theater.

A CEO does not need the full Omni-Lattice cascade on day one. They need to show a team that agent work can be reviewed. Portable skills make review possible. Diffs stay human-sized. FractiSkills is the luggage layer under that weather. If Lattice is the room, FractiSkills is what you carry into the room so the room does not have to rebuild the workshop every morning.

About 1.618 stays design language here — nesting depth grammar, not a compile flag. Keep that sentence loud and marketers stop smuggling magic into skill decks.

What a skill pack actually contains

In plain English: a name, a job, a short brief, pointers to local ground truth, and a refusal to roam when the seed is enough. That last line matters. Open-ended tool tours erase advantages. Roam theater burns tokens and confuses guests.

A good pack also names what it is not. Not clinical advice. Not a finished physics proof. Not a password store. Lite edges keep keys with the operator. BYOK means your key is your password — headers for requests, not a center that hoards secrets.

What fails when skills are not portable

When skills are not portable, every session renegotiates religion. Agents invent slang. Humans lose the thread. Diffs balloon. Guests feel clever fog instead of craft.

When skills are portable but overgrown, you get attic dumps with nicer filenames. Portability without MCA squeeze is just luggage that never leaves the porch. Crystallize. Animate only what the day requires. Squeeze nested clutter.

When skills are portable and thin, you get empty badges. A name without a job is marketing. A job without honesty posture is risk theater. A honesty posture without pointers is sermon.

FractiSkills aims at the Goldilocks middle: enough pack to travel, enough refusal to stop roam, enough reciprocity to tip. That middle is the product. Chrome doors without packs are costumes.
