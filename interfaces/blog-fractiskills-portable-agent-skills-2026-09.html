Portable agent skills for Lattice Chat.

SS Vibelandia — 2026-09-11 · Prudencio Mendez · Reno · FractiAI · Fair Exchange · with credit to Daniel Ari Friedman

Guests arrive with a question. Nested agents fan out across papers, protocols, QUESTFEST doors, and research suites. Somewhere in that scramble, most stacks either paste the whole site into every child prompt — or invent a shiny new door that pretends to be the product. FractiSkills does neither. It files one portable skill per surface — a thin SKILL.md brief — so Lattice Chat can point instead of dump, under the Infinite Octaves catalog layer.

FractiSkills as a named whole-site portable-skill program originates with Daniel Ari Friedman, Ph.D. (FractiAI · Active Inference Institute). This ship note and the SING 13 Lattice Chat companion are filed with credit to his original paper (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22712651) and repository (github.com/docxology/FractiSkills) — local registry corpus plus pointer-first load; not a republication of his live-crawl manuscript, and not a Skillarum embed.

What the local corpus actually does

FractiSkills walks monorepo registries, renders a SKILL.md package per surface under skills/<area>/<id>/, and writes skills/index.json. Regenerate with npm run generate:lattice-skills. The current local index locks around 421 packages — a structural count, not a prophecy that the public web was crawled into finished physics. Runtime returns at most eight pointers and pinches at most two skill bodies. That pinch is the product: enough orientation for a child agent, not a fat dump that melts the token margin.

Read the Infinite Octave AI Catalog Layer companion as the municipal filing office; FractiSkills is the drawer of portable labels inside that office. It is not a CMOS/tensor/master engine pin. AR4524 / AR4530 are filing characters only. Φ ≈ 1.618 nests shelves and skill pointers; it does not retire GPUs. Peer Moving up the stack for cool · harmonize · scale — FractiSkills is how Lattice Chat inherits briefs without pasting archives.

Why portable skills beat paste culture

Paste culture looks fast until nested agents multiply. Each child re-inlines the same whitepaper. Costs climb. Coherence drops. Soft Story and measured rails blur. Portable skills reverse that: one thin brief per surface, pointer-first retrieval, shared index, regenerate when the monorepo moves. Guests still get hospitality — a human ship-blog note first — while builders get a regenerable corpus that stays inside honesty rails.

What this note refuses: embedding Skillarum as if it were production infrastructure; treating a live 405-page public crawl as production proof; pinning FractiSkills into the Infinite Octaves engine shelf; claiming infinite GPU-free scaling because skills are thin. What it affirms: local monorepo SKILL.md packages, pointer-first Lattice Chat load, credit to Daniel’s original program, Fair Exchange when the orientation delivered.
