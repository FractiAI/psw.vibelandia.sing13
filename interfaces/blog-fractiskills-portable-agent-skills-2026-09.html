What FractiSkills is

Picture the whole SS Vibelandia monorepo as a city of surfaces: whitepapers in docs/, ship-blog notes, protocols, research suites, Lattice product routes, Core meta briefs. FractiSkills walks those registries, renders a SKILL.md package per surface under skills/<area>/<id>/, and writes skills/index.json. One skill, one surface. Frontmatter carries id, tags, routes, sources. The body stays short — operational honesty, not a second whitepaper.

Regenerate with npm run generate:lattice-skills when registries move. The current local index locks around 421 packages — a structural count for the wet lab, not a prophecy that the public web was crawled into finished physics. Curated Lattice and Core meta skills stay in the tree beside generated leaves so the valet still knows honesty boundaries and product surfaces by name.

That is operationally boring in the best Reno way. Nested agents inherit pointers. Lattice Chat’s cool · harmonize · scale pitch stays coherent. Nobody pastes four hundred skill bodies into a child loop and calls it intelligence. The corpus is local, regenerable, and honest about what it is not.

How Lattice Chat loads skills

Runtime lives in lib/lattice-skills.mjs. Match the guest ask against the index. Return at most eight pointers. Pinch at most two skill bodies (short excerpts). That is the Goldilocks budget: enough texture to orient a nested child, not enough paste to pretend the library is the prompt.

Parents metabolize the ask (MCA). Children inherit the pointer pack. Peer-firewall stays on — children do not mesh-sync the full corpus with each other. When the ask drifts to zero, scale the skill load to zero. Guests on Lattice Chat get hospitality first: a human ship note, then whitepaper, then runnable suite — Nevada product order.
