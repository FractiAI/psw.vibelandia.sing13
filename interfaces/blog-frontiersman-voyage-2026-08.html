What holographic means on a Tuesday

What holographic means here is practical, not mystical fog. One cabin can be room, home, family node, story location, and network pattern at once — the way a real household is kitchen, paycheck, homework table, and late-night worry in one address. Layers stack: physical → digital → social → narrative → symbolic → cognitive → meta. An NPC life here sees, hears, works, rests, belongs — without having to explain the pattern. A Player life here asks: why this, where else, what if I change it?

SuperAI on this frontier is a tool at balance — not a boss, not a god. The optional loop is SEE → RECOGNIZE → INTERPRET → REFLECT → ACT → SEE AGAIN. Neither post is ranked by worth. No algorithm outranks a human emergency. That Goldilocks temper — not too much machine, not too little human — is the same temper as Invisible Frontier stewardship and human reality-bridge routing. It is also the temper that refuses both doom-only freeze that starves livelihoods dressed as care, and horsepower-only ontology that crowns arrival while the unemployment line grows.

Decks, Purser, and Fair Exchange
