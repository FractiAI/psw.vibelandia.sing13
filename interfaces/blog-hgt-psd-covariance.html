Chromatin maps that stay mathematically legal

SS Vibelandia — 2026-06-17 · Fair Exchange

Predicting 3D chromatin conformation is not only a biology problem — it is a geometry problem. Contact maps that wander outside the positive semi-definite (PSD) cone are mathematically illegal as covariances, no matter how pretty the heatmap looks. Hierarchical Genomic Tokenization with structured PSD covariance operators (HGT-PSD) treats Hi-C prediction as a Conditional PSD Covariance Estimator so predicted contacts stay inside the legal cone by construction.

Laboratories and computational teams have spent a decade racing leaderboards on Hi-C regression. The quieter failure mode is downstream: a tool assumes a valid covariance, a pipeline assumes distance decay that still makes sense, and a pretty matrix that is not PSD silently poisons everything that follows. FractiAI’s HGT-PSD paper puts the constraint in the architecture instead of hoping a post-hoc repair will catch it.

PSD by construction, not by apology

The model maps genomic sequence to a sequence-dependent basis Φ(x) and defines contact adjacency as Ŷ(x) = Φ(x)Φ(x)ᵀ + diag(σ²(x)) — a Gram matrix plus a non-negative diagonal. Proposition 1 is the short proof that every prediction resides in S₊ⁿ. That is the mathematical legality the title promises: you do not “usually” stay PSD; you stay PSD because the estimator is built that way.

Stationary distance decay and physical contact structure still matter — biology is not optional — but the paper’s punchline for operators is that physical constraints belong in the operator, not only in the loss poem. When downstream methods treat the matrix as a covariance, legality is not a nice-to-have; it is the difference between science and decorative heatmaps.

Hierarchy from 250 kb down to 10 kb

Scaling uses hierarchical genomic tokenization from 250-kb bins down to 10-kb bins. The hierarchy is not a branding flourish. Coarse tokens stabilize long-range structure; fine tokens recover local detail; training uses an Orthogonal Frobenius-Space Masking Loss across distance-stratified regimes so the model does not cheat by over-fitting one distance band while pretending to understand another.

Hi-C regression on ENCODE GM12878 matrices is a crowded benchmark field. Guarantees that outputs remain PSD are rarer than leaderboard brags. A method that keeps physical constraints in the architecture reduces silent failures when the next lab tool assumes a valid cone. Distance strata and silent failure modes

Contact prediction fails in layers. Long-range contacts can look plausible while local loops rot. Local loops can glitter while compartments dissolve. Distance-stratified masking exists because a single Frobenius average will happily hide those failures inside a “good enough” score. Orthogonal masking across regimes is the paper’s way of forcing the model to earn legality and structure at more than one length scale.

Silent failure is the enemy of reproducible genomics. A heatmap that is not PSD can still look publishable. A downstream covariance consumer will not care about your poster. HGT-PSD’s architectural bet is that legality belongs upstream — Gram-plus-diagonal — so apology patches become rarer. That does not mean biology is solved.
