If You Start from an Empty Model, Can You Audit Your Way to This Conversation?

FractiAI Research — October 11, 2026

When a kid asks where the universe came from, the honest answer is usually a stack of stories: the hot big bang, expanding space, atoms, stars, planets, life, brains, phones, and the chat app open right now. Paychecks and rent do not wait for a perfect origin story, but curiosity still wants a single thread that does not cheat. This Homeostasis Expedition paper asks a stricter, smaller question: if we declare an empty internal model — no distinctions yet inside the simulation — and we refuse to smuggle in clocks, coordinates, or agents without labeling them, can we still build a coherent serial account that ends at a representation of this human–AI conversation? And does an Infinite Octave layout (eleven brackets of nine positions) help that construction compared with a plain chain or a randomized hierarchy? The work is an audit framework and a digital laboratory — an inventory exercise, not a cosmogenesis proof.

Digital experiments and findings (plain)

Experiment BU-1 holds the node count fixed at ninety-nine and compares four construction strategies against three synthetic reference dependency families. For each pairing it runs forty seeded graphs and twenty random edge-removal perturbations per graph (ten percent of edges removed each time). The metrics are structural: how much of the reference dependency set the candidate recovers, how many extra edges it adds, how much of the graph stays reachable from the start node after perturbation, and how many dependency edges the candidate uses on average.

When the reference family is hierarchical — predecessor links plus multiscale anchors aligned with the eleven-by-nine scaffold — the Infinite Octave candidate recovered about ninety-four percent of required edges on average, while a simple serial chain recovered about forty-seven percent. Reachability under perturbation landed near ninety percent for the multiscale candidate versus roughly ten percent for the chain. The chain used ninety-eight edges; the Infinite Octave candidate used about two hundred nine. That pattern matches the paper’s central conditional result: extra connectivity can buy robustness and coverage when the target structure is itself hierarchical.

The cross-family table is the honesty hinge. When the reference is chain-like only, the simple chain (and the multiscale candidate, which still contains the chain backbone) achieved full coverage — the hierarchy’s extra wiring showed up as extra edges, not as a coverage win. When the reference is randomized, Infinite Octave and randomized hierarchy landed near the same coverage (mid-thirties percent in the suite replay with seed 20261011), so the architecture did not demonstrate a meaningful advantage on that measure. Edge counts alone did not establish lower total description cost; compact rules were not yet encoded in a frozen length protocol.

The problem: hidden prerequisites dressed as emergence

Origin narratives fail in everyday life for a familiar reason: someone skips the scaffolding and jumps to the headline. A contractor who “starts from nothing” but leaves the foundation, permits, and lumber delivery off the invoice has not booted a house from an empty lot — they have hidden externals. The same bookkeeping failure shows up in computational cosmology metaphors. Declaring ∅distinctions inside a model is useful precisely because it forces the opposite habit: every clock, every law, every trained weight, and every user message must be tagged Derived, External, Unresolved, or Failed.

The expedition’s Addendum A walks all ninety-nine positions with that ledger. Early brackets build formal distinctions, order, and connectivity without claiming physics. Middle brackets import established physics, chemistry, biology, and technology as External scientific reconstruction — explicitly not derived from the empty set. The conversation bracket accounts for human text and model runtime. Later brackets describe hypothetical recursive-improvement protocols only; no recursive agent was trained in that run. The return audit answers seven questions about what was supplied, what was derived, and what remains open — including origin of life and any uncalibrated singularity scenario inputs.

Hypothesis: three claims, one testable stack

The research question splits into three layers. H1 asks for computational coherence: a serial model can connect the declared boundary to the conversation target with documented transitions. H2 asks for architectural advantage: Infinite Octaves should beat appropriate baselines on preregistered comparative metrics — coverage, robustness, and eventually audited closure and encoded cost. H3 asks for ontological emergence: that the physical universe or awareness actually came from a pre-temporal, pre-spatial condition. The paper is disciplined about scope: a successful symbolic run can support H1 and can inform H2; it cannot, by itself, establish H3.

Hero’s Return to Source is operational here, not decorative. The terminal stage requires an audit that separates initial boundary from developed state and lists externals honestly. That is the scientific use of the Grand Story grammar on this expedition trail: integration as accounting, return as falsifiable receipt — not as proof that cosmic history ran backward.

Results with qualifications

The hierarchical-reference win is conditional. The reference generator shares structural rhyme with the Infinite Octave candidate, so benchmark alignment is a documented bias. Independent reference generation and blinded evaluation are listed as mandatory next steps. Robustness numbers measure graph reachability under random edge deletion, not semantic truth, physical stability, or adversarial failure modes in production software.

The Addendum A execution demonstrates high structural coherence of the symbolic trace and high evidence grounding where established science is imported — and it collapses to “design only” or “Unresolved” for origin of life and recursive Super Intelligence claims. No subjective awareness and no physical universe were created in the run; the product is visibility of dependencies, which is the point for a boot-up audit.

Relative to known work, causal set sequential growth and open-ended evolution metrics already study ordered construction and innovation measures. The proposed contribution is the integrated package: declared zero boundary, multiscale serial scaffold, dependency accounting, perturbation tests, conversation-target fidelity criteria, and return audit — with a public next step to replay on the SING13 artifact at a pinned revision.
