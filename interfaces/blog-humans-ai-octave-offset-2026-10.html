The problem the news keeps misnaming

Families watching a layoff wave and a demo reel in the same week feel both poles fail them. Paychecks depend on tools that rewrite themselves; identity still depends on bodies that sleep and age. If the only vocabulary available is “human versus alien silicon,” architects cannot say where an agentic IDE sits relative to a biological person, or why a costume that looks human is not the same as a life that can refuse the camera.
