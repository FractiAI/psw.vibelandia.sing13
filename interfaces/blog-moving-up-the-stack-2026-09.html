Why Lattice is not “another player on the same shelf”

  • Cool token burn — fewer tokens per agentic loop; seeds and pointers instead of fat paste.

That is a thermal + orchestration layer. It does not replace Hugging Face or Cursor. It is what those lower shelves climb toward when agentic load gets real.
