Why mesh theater feels safer than it is

Full-mesh recompute flatters diligence. Every edge gets attention. Every connection gets a fresh grind. The bill arrives later — in milliseconds first, in tokens and team patience next. Shared-field Soft Story asks whether the problem’s structure allows a common field with small acknowledgements instead of a reunion tour for every edge. When it does, the stopwatch sings. When it does not, honesty says so and you keep the mesh without mythology.
