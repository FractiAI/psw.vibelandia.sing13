We timed two ways of thinking in silicon. One was faster.

SS Vibelandia — 2026-08-13 — plain speak · Fair Exchange

We timed two ways of doing the same sandbox work: redo everything on every connection (legacy mesh), versus share one field and send small phase-acks (MRI-style). The clock is real. The magnet is not.

Honesty first: these are Node Bloch + optional KomaMRI.jl CPU timings on one host. Not clinical TR/TE. Not CUDA invoices. Not proof that Synthio displaces hyperscale data centers or that Synthio is the 99 Octave engine identity.

What the stopwatch said

On the shipped receipt, the shared-field arm was much cheaper than per-edge recompute — hundreds of times on the Node Bloch lane, tens of times on the live KomaMRI lane. Host-dependent. Re-run the suite to refresh the milliseconds. Receipts live next to the paper.
