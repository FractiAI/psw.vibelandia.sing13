Why speed-only routing fails the corridor

Speed-only systems treat the street as an infinite buffer. Drivers absorb the wait. Riders absorb the surge. Servers absorb the thrash. Towns absorb the congestion. Someone always pays — often the household least able to. Goldilocks routing asks a prior question: is this trip in band? If density is too sparse, waiting forever is not kindness. If load is too crushed, packing tighter is not hospitality. The middle keeps people moving without turning the corridor into a bruise.

Published transportation tables keep the arithmetic reproducible. Nested agents inherit shared nouns: band, density, timing, load, accept, defer.
