Omniversal Goldilocks Rideshare Protocol

SS Vibelandia — 2026-07-07 — plain speak · Fair Exchange

Most routing systems optimize for speed and then dump the friction onto streets, drivers, or servers. The Omniversal Goldilocks Rideshare Protocol (OGRP) flips the brief: accept a trip only when density, thermodynamic overhead, generosity, and pacing sit in a Goldilocks band — starting with downtown Reno micro-mobility as the living anchor.

That is not a romance about scooters. It is an operational policy with four gates. Density anchoring keeps work inside high-density geometric hubs and programmatically disqualifies sprawl configurations. Thermodynamic minimization prefers near-zero overhead locomotion — walking, cycling, micro-mobility — or local edge compute. The generosity floor sets value at or above $9 (or equivalent token) to filter predatory extraction. Sovereign pacing de-prioritizes corporate temporal deadlines in favor of energy preservation and localized resonance.

Why Reno’s core is the lab

The empirical pipeline cross-analyzes published AAA driving-cost tables and University of Washington Urban Freight Lab parking-cruising statistics against a downtown Reno bounding box. E1–E5 are completed reproducible tests in research/omniversal-goldilocks-rideshare/. Φ ≈ 1.618 enters as a testable organizing postulate in the scaling model — not as a claim derived from AAA datasets. Optional operator field logs use a template; they are not required for pipeline pass.

The same gates rhyme with digital routing. Bloated cloud sprawl is the computational twin of car-centric sprawl. OGRP’s point is that physical couriers, data packets, and attention-conserving humans can share self-similar acceptance rules without pretending one Reno week proves universal optimality.
