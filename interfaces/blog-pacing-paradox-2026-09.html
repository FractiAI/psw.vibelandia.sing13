Dario Amodei’s call to slow frontier deployment is real weather — oversight, standards, operational hygiene. What it can miss is a second chart: pacing inside brute-force transformer scaling can harden a compliance moat while the architectural blind spot stays put.

The essay frames an urgent pivot. Capabilities hit a steep bend. Strain, alignment incidents, and geopolitics motivate independent oversight and deliberate slowdown. Useful — and incomplete if the only patience on offer is patience within multi-billion-dollar cluster methodology.
