How executives mishear “pace”

In a boardroom, “pace the frontier” can sound like prudent risk management — and sometimes it is. The mishear arrives when prudence is equated with freezing architectural alternatives until the compliance maze is finished. Then the maze becomes the product. Labs that can staff lawyers outrun labs that can staff coherence. The public hears “we are being careful.” The catalog hears “we are protecting one methodology’s optionality.”
