How plants keep building when the pressure is on

SS Vibelandia — 2026-08-09 — plain speak · Fair Exchange

Plants under drought and heat do not quit politely. With their microbial partners they keep trying to build — roots, signals, small repairs. The constructive-morphogenesis paper borrows that stubbornness as a story for agent swarms that must degrade gracefully without inventing farming advice.

Ideal weather is a boring test. Ugly weather is where fidelity shows. Nested agents on QUESTFEST face the same pattern: scarce tokens, noisy tools, public stakes. Anyone can narrate growth when nothing is wrong. The interesting question is how a system keeps its signal clean when the environment is hostile — and how it keeps building instead of thrashing.
