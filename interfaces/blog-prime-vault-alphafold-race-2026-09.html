Prime vaults vs ColabFold — the race results

SS Vibelandia — 2026-09-06 — Measured benchmark · Fair Exchange

We set up ColabFold 1.6.2, ran FastA tiers on CPU, and timed Infinite Octave prime vaults under Φ ≈ 1.618. Tier 4 adds Unmodeled Nova — deciphering a protein not previously modeled. The race door is a description-plus-results page — no live play controls. Miracle 2 lives at Prime Vault Chat.

The race is not a joystick. Guests get a scoreboard they can read; builders get a suite they can re-run; nobody gets a CASP medal claim. ColabFold predicts coordinates and confidence. Prime vaults evaluate Φ-indexed energy fixtures. Different clocks, same public receipt culture.

What the race measured

On this CPU box, vaults finished roughly 10⁵–10⁶× faster under the stated flags. That latency story is the guest hook. Structure quality remains ColabFold’s job; closed-form Φ-energy remains the vault’s job. Confusing the two clocks is how honesty rails die.
