Why pages need a prelude

First viewport clutter kills brand. House rules already refuse dashboard heroes, floating badges, and pill clusters. Soundtrack preludes add presence without adding clutter: motion and music as atmosphere, not as a second article. Guests feel the room before they parse the argument.
