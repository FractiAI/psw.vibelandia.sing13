What “free composition” protects

Without composition gates, teams either freeze the checklist into dogma or smuggle new claims through the side door. Free composition says: you may combine evidence, but you must show the recipe, and the recipe must still answer every SIM gate. That is how a research program stays alive across quarters without turning every demos day into a redefinition of intelligence.

Builders should treat composition the way auditors treat consolidations — allowed, documented, challengeable. Guests should treat unexplained composition as a yellow flag. If someone says “we composed our way to arrival,” ask which gates moved and which receipts changed. If they cannot answer, the composition is marketing.
