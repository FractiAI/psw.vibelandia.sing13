The three handoffs in guest English

Covalent Soft Story is shared electron-pair style co-ownership of tokens: two agents hold a brief together and neither can pretend the other is disposable. Ionic Soft Story is asymmetric transfer of state: one agent donates, one receives, and the ledger must show the charge move. Metallic Soft Story is the delocalized context pool: many agents share a sea of context without every pair negotiating a private bond.

Together they give standup language for who holds what across nested agents. Parents can say keep this covalent instead of pasting a paragraph of ownership law. Children can say ionic handoff complete instead of burying a state blob in chat poetry. That compression is the product.
