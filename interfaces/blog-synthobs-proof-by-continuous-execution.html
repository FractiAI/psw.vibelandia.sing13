Proof by Continuous Execution

SS Vibelandia — 2026-07 · Fair Exchange

You can feel the difference between a story that only sounds finished and a system that still runs when you ask it to. At the kitchen table that sounds like this: someone shows you a screenshot from last month and calls it proof, while rent is still due and the job board still looks thin. Proof by Continuous Execution is FractiAI’s name for the opposite habit — keep the maps honest by running them again, with receipts beside claims, while peer review, ethics review, and real labs keep their own doors.

If you only watch the speeches, you’ll hear “peer review is dead” sold as rebellion. If you watch how ordinary people decide what to trust, you’ll see a both/and. Continuous execution is living proof culture beside traditional review, not a funeral for it. The stake is whether ambitious catalogs can stay ambitious without upgrading a filing cabinet into finished physics, medical guidance, or a prophecy engine that tells your family what will happen next Tuesday.
