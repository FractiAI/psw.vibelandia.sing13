What if the collider fit on a workbench?

SS Vibelandia — 2026-08-23 — plain speak · Fair Exchange

CERN’s ring is huge for a reason: smash particles hard enough and you need distance, magnets, and power. This paper asks a different question for our library — can we file that footprint as one scale among 99 octaves, and sketch what a table-top “equivalent talk” would look like under Φ ≈ 1.618?

Honesty first: this is a catalog map and proposed lab sketches. It does not claim a benchtop already matches multi-TeV beams, retire superconducting rings, or authorize unsupervised RF / pulsed-power work. Φ is design language — not a physics monopoly.

Three pieces on the bench (cartoon)

An octave-tuned cavity (instead of kilometers of linac talk), a small flux-rope injector (instead of mountains of dipoles talk), and a solid-state pick-up cell (instead of cathedral detectors talk). Useful as a filing triad — not a procurement checklist.
