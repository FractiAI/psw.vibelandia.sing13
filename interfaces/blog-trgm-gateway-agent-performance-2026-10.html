Do Agents Do Better With a Tiny Reality Gateway Than Without One?

FractiAI Research — October 25, 2026

A GPS unit changes a road trip even when the car stays the same. Parents checking a school portal already know the difference between a student with a study plan and one improvising under a pile of tabs. After the Tiny Reality Generation Model locked a ten-bits-per-second biological reality stream pipe into its architecture, the next expedition asked a practical follow-on: if agents face the same tasks, does routing them through that tiny-reality gateway change how they score compared with running without it?

The problem of a gateway that never meets a scoreboard

Generators can look complete on a shelf and still fail the first day someone asks what they do for workers who have to finish a checklist before rent is due. TRGM already cleared stack coherence, genetic cataloguing, predictive validation, and a reality catalog. What it had not yet done was put agents on a paired course—same tasks, two arms—and publish whether the gateway earns its keep. Without that scoreboard, “tiny reality” stays a brochure. With it, the Homeostasis Expedition gets a follow-on ledger that can be wrong in public.

What we actually ran

The laboratory defines two treatments before any score is read. With-gateway agents receive the TRGM scaffold: the ten-bits-per-second biological reality stream pipe, a structured three-symbol stream mapped to wave, node, and fq, polygon-spin clocking, an Infinite Octave rhyme tree, and folded DNA theater. Without-gateway agents get unstructured noise, a flat encode, an unfolded polymer, and periodic null probes—no bio pipe. Both arms face the same five tasks: navigate a tiny reality, hold Goldilocks viability, preserve stack coherence, recover triad channel labels on held-out non-gene features, and take information under a rate budget. Twelve trials run per arm under validation seed twenty twenty-six ten twenty-five two. Factory floors already A/B tool setups across shifts; this lab does that for digital agents.

Hypothesis under test

If the gateway is more than branding, with-gateway agents should clear pre-registered margins over without-gateway fixtures on navigation success, viability rate, mean coherence, and channel balanced accuracy, while locking the ten-bits-per-second pipe on the gateway arm alone. The expedition refuses to treat a live multi-provider language-model contest or a wet genetics claim as this paper’s job. The claim is narrower and still falsifiable: on this digital task battery, does the gateway arm win?

What the paired run found

Under seeds twenty twenty-six ten twenty-five and twenty twenty-six ten twenty-five two, experiments E1 through E8 passed eight of eight. With-gateway agents reached one hundred percent navigation success and one hundred percent viability with mean coherence one point zero and channel balanced accuracy one point zero, bio pipe locked at ten bits per second. Without-gateway agents landed near sixty-seven percent navigation and viability, mean coherence about zero point two six, and channel balanced accuracy about zero point three eight, with no bio pipe. Paired deltas were about plus thirty-three percentage points on navigation and viability, plus zero point seven four on coherence, and plus zero point six three on channel recovery. Kids sorting laundry already know labeled drawers beat a dumped pile; the channel and coherence gaps rhyme with that everyday contrast, measured on the stack rather than on a sermon.

The without arm was not a cartoon zero. Some unstructured driven trials still cleared navigation when luck and geometry lined up. The paper claims the paired advantage under pre-registered gates, not that every baseline agent always fails. Paychecks and grant cycles both punish overclaiming a baseline into oblivion.

What this is for on the expedition trail

Homeostasis Expedition already carries TRGM as engine companion fifty and the predictive catalog as fifty-one. This follow-on is an application companion—published, standalone, linked from the journey page—and deliberately not an engine pin. It gives the trail a with-versus-without agent scoreboard so later stages can ask whether live agents still need the gateway, or whether a thinner scaffold is enough. Tuition bills punish expeditions that only collect metaphors; a paired ledger is the cheaper habit.

Framework value without a victory lap

Beating a flat and unstructured baseline on coherence is a modest architectural win. Matching a feature-kind grammar against random channel labels is expected when the gateway carries that grammar—and still worth reporting beside navigation and viability. The ten-bits-per-second pipe remains architectural bandwidth named in the honesty rail, not a clinic EEG claim. Commuters stuck behind a stalled shuttle already understand software confidence that outruns clearance; the same caution applies to calling this a universal agent ranking.
