How stages and sandboxes fail without three bowls

One-bowl failure modes are familiar. Host and swarm share one context until the host cannot hear themselves. Spill telemetry sits in the same buffer as the root brief until noise looks like signal. Guests feel either crushed at the core or lost beyond any horizon. Three-bowl Soft Story does not magically fix venues; it gives operators nouns for diagnosing which bowl failed.

Ask: is the core too large (host drowning in swarm paste)? Is the amphitheater missing (no Goldilocks immersion layer)? Is the horizon absent (no spill containment, so telemetry floods the stage)? Those questions are cheaper than rebuilding the entire demo stack.
