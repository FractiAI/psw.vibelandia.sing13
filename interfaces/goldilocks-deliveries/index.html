The Goldilocks Experience

What's it mean?

Goldilocks is where win-win-wins converge — not too much, not too little for all parties, including the environment.

  • Core-centric service areas — high-density hubs where runs are short, human, and worth doing without sprawl markup.
  • E-scooters — good for the environment and good for the wallets of our Valet Franchisees.
  • Guests who get it — we work with people who understand and appreciate these things, plus our unconventional, old-school approach.
  • Stay in your Goldilocks — all parties keep their rhythm instead of interrupting it to push a maximized-profit transaction for shareholders.

How the channel works

  1. Email Valet Pru to join — as guest, purveyor, or Goldilocks Valet franchisee. One email, one human answer. No forms, no app store, no account.
  2. Accepted? You're in the channel — we place you in the WhatsApp group for your channel, where requests broadcast and franchisees accept.
  3. Order from your purveyor — place your order directly with the kitchen, cafe, store, or shop of your choice. Book a Valet to pick up and deliver, or ask us to handle everything for 18% of the total bill.
  4. Broadcast your pickup + tip offer — tell the channel what to pick up, where, and your valet tip offer (floors: $9 run · $16.18/hr · $161.80/day). A franchise valet accepts what fits.
  5. All else handled offline — procurement, delivery, and payment ride the established rails (Venmo · PayPal · Cash App), honor system, Fair Exchange.

The floors below are courtesy minimums ($9 runs · $16.18/hr · $161.80/day). Your tip offer above the floor is what makes the exchange sing — and what makes a node say yes.
