Why a frontier expedition now

Recent recursive-fidelity work refused a magic-number crown and refused a multi-constant controller rescue. Beside those locked baselines, the ship keeps asking a quieter question: can complementary structure — continuous change, proportional coherence, and containment — produce a Goldilocks regime of useful evolution with bounded drift? The Homeostasis Expedition is the guest door onto that question.
