Homeostasis Expedition

Canoe the living river — evolution without dissolution, balance without freeze

SS Vibelandia now offers a dedicated frontier focus: the Homeostasis Expedition. It is not a cabin and not a deck claim. It is a curated journey through the ship’s research trail on recursive balance — how systems keep changing without losing themselves, and how “just right” can mean a moving band rather than a still point.

Picture the trip as an Amazon river canoe: dense canopy on both banks, a current that never stops, and a craft that stays upright by moving with the water. That image is the voyage metaphor for the papers linked below. Rent still comes due. Job descriptions still rewrite themselves. Tools that improve themselves still drift. This expedition gathers the notes that ask whether stability can be dynamic — and reports the nulls as honestly as the wins.

Human emergency outranks algorithms. Belonging remains voluntary. Fair Exchange runs through the Purser when utility lands.

Why a frontier expedition now

Recent recursive-fidelity work refused a magic-number crown and refused a multi-constant controller rescue. Beside those locked baselines, the ship keeps asking a quieter question: can complementary structure — continuous change, proportional coherence, and containment — produce a Goldilocks regime of useful evolution with bounded drift? The Homeostasis Expedition is the guest door onto that question. It ties the engine companions (Holographic Homeostasis · Goldilocks ≡ Net Zero) to the exploratory e×φ×prime run, without rewriting any prior scoreboard.
