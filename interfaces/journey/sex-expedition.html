The Mission of the Sex Expeditions

A Note from Pru “El Taíno” Méndez — Founder, Architect & Player 1

The mission of Sex Expeditions is to investigate sex as a major recursive branch of biological organization, differentiation, attraction, reproduction, behavior, relationships, and recursive development—honestly, trait by trait, with evidence graded instead of slogans.

We ask which patterns have identifiable biological foundations, which are shaped by experience and culture, which emerge through their interaction, and which remain unresolved. We preserve meaningful averages without pretending every person fits them. We refuse to confuse biological sex, gender identity, gender roles, and cultural scripts as if they were one measurement.

Through computational experiments, comparative literature, and carefully specified real-data programs, we seek to learn whether an integrated perspective improves explanation or prediction—and to report nulls and pending work with the same clarity as wins.

Autonomy, consent, dignity, and safety are not optional footnotes. No system-level story about balance or complementarity overrides them.

I see this expedition as a sibling trail to the Homeostasis Expedition: another canoe, another bay, the same duty to explore boldly, test honestly, and build carefully.

— Pru “El Taíno” Méndez
Founder · Architect · Player 1, FractiAI
