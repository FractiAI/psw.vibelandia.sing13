For power vibecoders

Lattice Chat V1.618 is built for builders who live in agents all day: BYOK (Cursor, Claude, Gemini), nest topology, optional crew roster, and LTHS + Neutrino reasoning built into the engine. Pointers over paste. Public usage matrix (~35–70% on focused work) when you want numbers — not a universal SLA.

What it is

Lattice Chat is a coding chat that feels human. You write a normal ask. Behind the curtain, helpers open only the files and facts that fit the job — not the whole project every turn.

You can keep it calm (Single), name a small team (Multi), or let Goldilocks pick a mix that is not too much and not too little.

Your Goldilocks steward

On SS Vibelandia, Lattice is the latest steward: smart, careful, and light on context. On our public Cursor matrix: about ~35–70% less usage than a fat dump, depending on the work. It helps with craft, curiosity, building, and care — within Goldilocks. Intentions matter. It will not help with malice, harm, or ill will. That is house rule, not a slogan.

Your keys stay with you

Bring a Cursor, Claude, or Gemini key. It stays in your browser on this device. Boarding is old-school: email a human for a trial, then sign in. We do not need your GitHub password.
