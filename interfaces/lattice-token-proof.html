What we compared

We ran one realistic multi-part coding question — map docs, UI, APIs, and a nested plan — under two loading styles:

  • Standard agentic (fat context) — paste large slices of docs, protocols, and code into the model, then re-use that fat context across map → plan → synthesize.
  • Lattice Chat V1.618 — a small nested team with RAG-style pointers and a short history window, instead of dumping the corpus.

What the difference means

Tokens are the fuel of AI coding agents. Dump-everything mode treats the project as one meal. Lattice treats it like a library: the lead agent points helpers at the shelves they need, then folds answers back together. On this bench, that is the measured mechanism — nesting and selection — not a fractal compression algorithm.

Absolute difference on this receipt is large because the fat path pastes volume and reuses it.

Claim boundary

  • Nested + pointers can beat fat dump-everything on estimated context size.
  • Nested can still cost more than a strong selected-context baseline.
  • SING φ / “fractal” wording is scale grammar and naming — not the savings engine.
