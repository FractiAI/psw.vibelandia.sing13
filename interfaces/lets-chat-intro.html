Let's Chat

Guest-to-guest encrypted comms on the ship — without becoming a Lattice Chat power user first.

Let's Chat is the Goldilocks comms lounge for registered guests and creators alike. Creators are guests here too. You do not need a provider API key, a repo workspace, or the full Lattice Chat agent stack — only an email the Purser has already seated.

Why Let's Chat

  • No harvesting — the center pipe relays encrypted envelopes for seconds, then they expire. No social graph mining. No ad profile.
  • Fractal EGS encryption — messages derive keys from the EGS frontal constant (Φ ≈ 1.618) on your device. The server sees ciphertext only.
  • Goldilocks environment — consent-first. Fair Exchange. Do Not Disturb when you need quiet. Predators never welcome.
  • Standalone or Collaborate — open /lets-chat directly, or hop over from Lattice Collaborate on Deck 8 Veranda.
