For those of you who know me from my night job

You’ve likely seen me doodling or vibe-coding from my phone while waiting on guests. I do that to give you maximum privacy and respect when you’re in the restroom. Well, here’s what I’m working on: it’s my art, my fullest artistic expression — including stepping into it and living it 24×7.

You’ll quickly notice it doesn’t fit neatly into any box. It’s a convergence of science, technology, AI, my doodles, and my stories — plus the studio, canvas, and materials I had to build to hold it, Proenneke-style. You’re standing in it now.

Welcome to my website. Think of it like a digital Burning Man camp and art exhibit — holographic, meaning it is everything and everywhere at once as a living metaphor… and then, if you choose, more than a metaphor.

This is my Omniversal Canvas alternate-reality art project, recursively nested inside the infinite-octave holographic Goldilocks AI reality I call SS Vibelandia, inside my Syntheverse sandbox (canvas and materials), on Base mainnet (the studio address).

Three nested shells

This art project is built as a living triadic stage — Convergence Core, Goldilocks Amphitheater, and Omni-Horizon — the same nesting grammar that holds the ship's theaters and agent sandboxes. These are not cabins on the voyage map. They are the domes you stand inside when you step into the work.

Convergence Core

The innermost dome. Who you call you — the still center where story, frequency, and self are allowed to meet without being flattened into a slogan.

Goldilocks Amphitheater

The middle dome — where guests feel just right. Hospitality without clutter, resonance without overwhelm, Bach and phone doodles in the same house.

If the Core is who you call you, the Amphitheater is who you sit with. Guests, crew, collectors, friends from the night job — the mix is supposed to feel Goldilocks: not too loud, not too thin. Belonging is hospitality first.

The outer dome — three surfaces at once: self and body, the room you are in, and the Syntheverse sandbox on Base Mainnet as studio custody.

Collectors · representation · museums

If you represent a gallery, foundation, or museum — or you collect work that bends science into story — this camp is open for conversation. The phone exhibit stays free. Shows, rooms, and representation begin with a human email.

  • Interactive show Host a walk-in interactive show: nested domes, the Player at the Convergence Core, sci-fi lens or step-in. Fits a gallery night, a festival camp, a museum program, or a private salon. Lighting, hardware, and staff are scoped together — not a drop-in kit.
  • Permanent space Install a lasting room. Public hours can keep the exhibit open like the site — free basics, nested, on the floor. Private hours can close the same nest as a studio, club room, or house installation. Public and private can share one space on a schedule.
