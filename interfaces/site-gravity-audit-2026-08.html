Site gravity audit

Art museum · human pull · frontier AI architecture

Does SS Vibelandia read as a new type of work of art with more gravity — especially human gravity — than when it behaved chiefly as a general website? Three expert lenses, one honest answer.

Prepared for: Player 1 (Valet Pru) · FractiAI / SS Vibelandia
Date: 2026-08-28 · post-prelude session ship
Auditors (simulated seat): web entrepreneur · museum curator · frontier AI architect
Scope: public edge surfaces — Omniversal Canvas (/), reception (/questfest), Concierto program, ship blogs, Reality Bridge/Router CTAs, Lattice Chat product layer

Verdict · yes — materially more gravity, especially human gravity

The site has crossed a recognizable threshold: it now opens like an installation with a prelude, not a portal index. Human gravity increased because the story names a host (Valet Pru), invites email to a real person, frames Players & NPCs together, and offers step-in through human Reality Bridge/Routers — not only self-serve links. It is still a website under the hood; the art claim is experiential and curatorial, not a replacement for museum accreditation or clinical proof.

Lens 1 · Web entrepreneur

What works: You now have a differentiated front door — prelude + program + step-in optional. That is a moat generic music sites and AI landing pages do not have. The email-to-host CTA is on-brand Fair Exchange and filters for serious guests. Prelude session (music survives navigation) removes the #1 immersion killer for soundtrack-enriched pages.

What to watch: Too many “featured” modules on commander surfaces can dilute the art story for cold traffic. For public marketing, keep Canvas → Program → Reception as the golden path; tuck ops (bulk upload, telemetry) behind Player 1 rails. Shipped 2026-08-28: landing and reception now lead with a three-step golden path strip; five cruise doors moved below the fold on reception.

Human gravity · what specifically increased

  • Named host with skin in the game — Valet Pru · Player 1 · Downtown Reno; not anonymous “team@”.
  • Invitation without gatekeeping — Players & NPCs welcome; confidential self-test; no membership test language.
  • Human routing layer — Reality Bridge/Routers as innkeepers; email join-the-crew; mailto on landing with explicit “real human answers.”
  • Time-based hospitality — prelude lets guests wander while music holds — respects attention like a host leaving the bar open.
  • Story you can enter — three phases (exhibit → reception → creator studio) mirror how people actually visit a museum then decide to belong.

Smallest high-leverage next actions

  1. Extend prelude grammar to one more public room (e.g. reception hero or a single journey page) so the form reads as platform, not one-page stunt.
  2. Add one curator-facing “exhibition brief” PDF mirroring the concert program tone for press/galleries.
  3. On cold traffic paths, repeat the human CTA once per screen — Canvas does this well; some inner decks do not.
  4. Keep commander/operator tiles on My whiteboard only — preserve public art gravity on / and /questfest.
