About Synthio

Synthio is the Syntheverse Sandbox agent for MRI simulation on a cloud-antenna story. It wraps the industry KomaMRI Bloch/k-space simulator (MRiLab companions) so creator seats can push toward empirical, safe, wet-style experimentation — without putting clinical RF into living tissue.

Cloud Services is this home: live activation, distributed compute outline (phantom sharding, Nblocks, Φ_EGS phase-lock), and launches into chat, dashboard, and papers.

Who can use it. Any Lattice allowlisted seat (creator or guest). Synthio reuses the Lattice Chat email and BYOK keys already on this device — guests do not sign in again. It is separate from the 99 Octave Omni-Lattice engine sync shelf.
