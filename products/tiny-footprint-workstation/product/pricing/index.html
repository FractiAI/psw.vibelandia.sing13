One shared world. Many perspectives. New discoveries. Faster convergence. Better decisions.

FractiSI is the shared-world intelligence and coordination infrastructure that enables agent fleets to move beyond independent answers toward collective discovery, composed understanding, and validated outcomes — powered by Tiny Footprint and Tiny Gateway™. Not merely shared-memory plumbing; not a low-cost Cursor or Claude substitute.

Still wins after gzip ~42%+ residual

Beats exact-text cache when words differ 4,758 vs 6,704 B

40 same-shape / different-words sessions: rhyme templates beat exact-text cache. Exact cache wins only identical text (332 B vs rhyme 5,867 B). Prefix reuse already helps exact prompts in production.

Aggregate representation cut 84.5% (≈80% research gate) · GPU alloc proxy 81.4% (288→53.6 MiB) · turn materialize ~9.6×. H100 rhyme-vs-prefix ~34% is simulation only.

Packaging rules

  1. Community is genuinely useful — no crippled core, no credit card to try.
  2. Don’t charge for model tokens — BYOK IDEs stay separate.
  3. Don’t charge per registration or per discovery — capacity + capability.
  4. Don’t make deployment mandatory — local compose + Git stay useful.
  5. Don’t charge for roadmap promises — sell Pro/Team/Enterprise when workflows are operational and supported.
  6. Enterprise must earn its contract — private deploy, governance, support, capacity.

Launch-stage honesty

Workstation 0.2 ships Community now (real-workload try; deploy optional). Discovery and convergence lab results justify the category and price revision; they do not by themselves prove every Pro/Team workflow is already productized. Adapters normalize IDE messages; they do not yet auto-drive live sessions.

  • Community available now.
  • Sell Developer Pro when paid intelligence features are operational and tested.
  • Sell Team when multi-user admin, collective discovery, and governance work.
  • Sell Enterprise through validated deployment contracts.
  • Don’t charge for roadmap promises.

Lab receipts stay qualified; reproduce on your hardware.

Annual & expansion

Annual commitments at the same effective monthly rate initially (no discount theater). Annual customers get defined support, capacity planning, priority updates, an architecture review, and predictable renewal. Capacity packs may follow as real workloads establish economics.
