## Abstract

**Background.** Economic value of scientific computation requires comparing the **complete productive capacity** of one competent human researcher-year in each environment — labor, infrastructure, discovery quality, validation, implementation, elapsed time.

**Objective.** Determine whether computation-first vs wet-lab-first produces more **substantive, independently validated** discoveries and **implemented progress** per dollar and calendar year; whether a **Discovery-to-Implementation Pipeline** increases conversion enough to justify cost.

**Methods.** Four conditions over **365 days**: (A) computation-first, no pipeline; (B) computation-first + pipeline; (C) wet-lab-first, no pipeline; (D) wet-lab-first + pipeline. Unit = one fully costed researcher-year (base **1,760** productive hours, sensitivity analyses). Matched portfolios, **≥200** repetitions prespecified, event-level funding and milestone records.

**Findings.** The comparison framework and measurable endpoints are **established**. Numerical findings on relative productivity, cost, implementation conversion, and time to impact are **not reported as established results** — independently auditable execution with complete project-level outputs **has not** been completed. Relative performance of the four conditions **remains undetermined**.

**Implications.** Computation becomes primary only where controlled evidence shows lower full cost or elapsed time for equivalent validated results without unacceptable reliability losses. Pipeline value = incremental outcomes vs incremental cost.

## 1–3. Introduction · research questions · experimental design

**Primary question:** For one competent researcher over 365 days, which environment yields greatest substantive **independently validated** discoveries at lowest **fully allocated** cost — computation-first or wet-lab-first?

**Secondary:** Does the **Discovery-to-Implementation Pipeline** increase implemented progress enough to justify incremental cost?

**Four conditions:**

| Condition | Environment | Pipeline |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| A | Human-led computation-first | No |
| B | Human-led computation-first | Yes |
| C | Human-led wet-lab-first | No |
| D | Human-led wet-lab-first | Yes |

Hypotheses **H1–H7** are falsifiable; none assumed true pre-experiment.
