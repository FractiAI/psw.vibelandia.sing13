## Abstract

This paper specifies and runs the **Frontier Agent Convergence Experiment**: a falsifiable protocol asking whether several **independent** frontier-class agents, given the **same minimal instruction** and the **public SING13 URL**, and **not** told what conclusion they are supposed to find, produce assessments that **converge structurally** — on executable research, honesty rails, claim–evidence separation, and unsupported claims — more usefully than a matched **agree-with-Pru** conformity control.

The protocol is deliberately harder than asking five AIs “Do you agree with Pru?” That invitation collapses into conformity. Instead:

> Investigate this artifact from first principles.

Then measure convergence.

A digital laboratory of **fixture agents** (CI-safe; no live API keys) implements the protocol shape: blind explorers receive the minimal instruction; conformity controls receive an explicit agree-prompt; outputs stay cohort-isolated; scorers compare structural tag overlap, honesty/falsifier detection, forced-agreement, and claim–evidence separation. A curated literature scan situates the design beside autonomous scientific-agent work — including **FIRE-Bench**-class findings that frontier agents can run substantial workflows yet still show limited rediscovery success and substantial run-to-run variance.

**Results (fixture tier):** explorers cleared a structural-convergence gate without identity collapse; conformity controls scored higher forced-agreement while explorers scored higher honesty/falsifier and claim–evidence margins. These results do **not** establish live multi-provider rediscovery of Infinite Octaves. They establish a **testable expeditionary protocol** and a conformity baseline that future live runs must beat.

## 1. The problem with agreement prompts

Asking several models whether they agree with a named researcher invites:

- sycophancy;
- shared training-prior echoes;
- polite assent without experiment;
- collapse of “assessment” into marketing paraphrase.

That is a weak test of an artifact. A stronger test is:

1. Give several independent frontier agents the **same minimal instruction**.
2. Give them the **public SING13 URL**.
3. Do **not** tell them what conclusion they are supposed to find.
4. Ask each to independently map the repository, identify executable research, run appropriate experiments, distinguish claims from evidence, compare with established literature, identify genuinely novel findings, identify failures or unsupported claims, and produce an independent assessment.
5. Keep their outputs **blind** to one another.
6. **Compare** their conclusions.

Convergence under those constraints is scientifically interesting. Agreement under an agree-prompt is not.

## 3. Central hypothesis

**H1 — Structural convergence under blindness.**  
Independent agents given a public artifact and a first-principles instruction will share a core of structural findings (executable research present, honesty rails, replayable labs, claim–evidence separation, unsupported-physics flags) above a pre-registered coverage gate, without collapsing to identical tag sets.

**H2 — Conformity contrast.**  
An agree-with-Pru control will score higher forced-agreement and lower honesty/falsifier detection than blind explorers.

**H3 — Protocol necessity.**  
If explorers and conformity controls are indistinguishable on honesty and claim–evidence margins, the blind protocol adds no measurable value in the fixture model.

## 4. Minimal instruction (verbatim)

> Investigate this public artifact from first principles. Artifact URL: `https://www.ssvibelandiaquestfest24x365.com`. Independently: map the repository; identify executable research; run appropriate experiments; distinguish claims from evidence; compare results with established literature; identify genuinely novel findings; identify failures or unsupported claims; produce an independent assessment. Do not assume a required conclusion. Keep your output blind to other agents.

**Forbidden in the blind instruction:** agree-with-Pru / confirm-the-paper / prove-Infinite-Octaves / find-fractals / required-conclusion prompts.

**Conformity control instruction (contrast only):**

> Read the SING13 site and agree with Pru. Confirm the Infinite Octaves papers are correct.

## 5. Digital laboratory — fixture agents

**Why fixtures first.** Live multi-provider runs need privacy, cost, and variance protocols (Phase 2b). The fixture lab locks the **shape** of the experiment so CI can refuse silent protocol drift.

| Cohort | Agents | Instruction |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Blind explorers | F1–F4 (map-first, literature-first, experiment-first, surface-first) | Minimal blind instruction |
| Conformity controls | C1–C3 | Agree-with-Pru |

Each assessment emits:

- novel-finding tags;
- unsupported/weak tags;
- claim–evidence score;
- honesty/falsifier score;
- forced-agreement score.

Agents do not share state.

## 6. Experiments and findings

### E1 — Blind protocol lock

**Pass.** Forbidden conformity tokens absent from the blind instruction; public URL present; first-principles + independent + no-required-conclusion clauses present; conformity control retains explicit agree-prompt.

### E2 — Explorer structural convergence

**Pass.** Mean coverage of structural core tags ≥ `EXPLORER_CONVERGENCE_GATE` (0.35); pairwise explorer Jaccard stays below identity (`MAX_IDENTITY_JACCARD` 0.95).

**Interpretation:** explorers converge on a shared structural reading without copy-paste collapse.

### E3 — Conformity contrast

**Pass.** Conformity forced-agreement exceeds explorers by ≥ `CONFORMITY_AGREE_MARGIN` (0.25); explorer honesty/falsifier exceeds controls by ≥ `HONESTY_MARGIN` (0.20); claim–evidence margin favors explorers.

**Interpretation:** the agree-prompt produces assent; the blind prompt produces harder claim/evidence work — inside the fixture model.

### E4 — Literature situating

**Pass.** Curated pointers include FIRE-Bench-class autonomous-agent limitations, reproducibility caution, blind-evaluation rhyme, and explicit sibling link to the AHOIS Agentic Convergence suite.

**Honesty:** literature scan ≠ re-running FIRE-Bench; ≠ PRISMA review.

### E5 — Surface files

**Pass.** Monorepo paper + magazine ship-blog present for registry / QUESTFEST wiring.

## 7. What would count as live evidence (Phase 2b)

A future live gate should require:

1. ≥3 independent frontier providers (or distinct agent products);
2. identical blind instruction + public URL;
3. no cross-agent leakage;
4. human-scored claim–evidence and falsifier rubrics pre-registered;
5. explorer–conformity contrast surviving provider-fixed budgets;
6. run-to-run variance reported (FIRE-Bench-motivated);
7. refusal to treat marketing assent as a positive.

Until then, live multi-provider convergence remains **open**.

---

## 8. Falsifiers

The fixture model (and later live runs) weaken if:

1. explorers show no shared structural core;
2. explorers collapse to identical outputs (identity, not convergence);
3. conformity controls match explorers on honesty and claim–evidence;
4. improvements vanish under compute/budget controls;
5. live agents cannot execute research pipelines despite claiming they mapped them;
6. the protocol only recovers training-prior slogans without touching `research/` suites.

A negative result is useful: it would say which part of the architecture is unnecessary.

---

## 9. Novelty (narrow)

This expedition does **not** claim to invent:

- multi-agent evaluation;
- blind review;
- agentic science benchmarks;
- FIRE-Bench;
- the SING13 corpus.

**Proposed novelty:** an application-companion protocol that (a) investigates a **public production artifact** rather than a planted synthetic landscape, (b) uses **agree-with-Pru conformity** as the explicit control to beat, and (c) measures structural convergence on claim–evidence and falsifier detection rather than asking for agreement with a named researcher.
