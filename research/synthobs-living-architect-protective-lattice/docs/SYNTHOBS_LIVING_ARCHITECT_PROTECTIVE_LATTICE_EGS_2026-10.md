## Abstract

This Homeostasis Expedition investigates whether Infinite Octaves can serve as a useful multiscale architecture across three construction problems: biological mineralized structures, recursive computational agents, and a coherent model from a declared net-zero boundary to a specific human–AI conversation.

The biological investigation compares coral skeleton formation and mollusk shell construction. The recursive-agent investigation specifies model revision under independent evaluation. The boot-up investigation carries forward previously reported synthetic graph-structural results for a 99-position architecture (11 × 9).

**Findings (current record).** Synthetic boot-up results report **88.3%** required-edge coverage and **97.5%** reachability under tested perturbations for Infinite Octaves vs a hierarchical reference, compared with **45.1%** coverage and **9.9%** robustness for a simple chain; mean **217.5** vs **98** dependency edges — **no** lower description cost established. Public coral and mussel datasets are **identified-not-harmonized**. No evidence establishes a single shared physical mechanism or derivation from absolute nothingness.

**Keywords:** Homeostasis Expedition; Infinite Octaves; coral biomineralization; mollusk shells; recursive self-improvement; net zero; dependency closure; Hero’s Return to Source; SING13; SynthOBS.
