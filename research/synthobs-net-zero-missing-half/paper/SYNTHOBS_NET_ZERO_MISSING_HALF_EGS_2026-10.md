## Abstract

This inaugural expedition investigates the complementary structure of reality within the Net Zero framework through mathematical proofs and computational experiments involving geometry, function decomposition, asymmetric equilibrium, material accounting, and hidden-variable inference.

**Findings (executed suite).** Circle and sphere decompositions reconstruct complete geometries; even/odd decomposition reconstructs sampled functions to floating-point precision; three unequal forces produce zero resultant; an unobserved accumulation completes a specified flow balance; multiple hidden configurations satisfy the same constraint; Fourier conjugate symmetry and phase-dependent wave cancellation confirm. **E1–E8 = 8/8** (seed `20261031`).

**Novelties.** Integration into a unified investigation of complementarity beyond symmetry; observational decomposition; distributed/asymmetric complementarity; proposed **Complementary Structure Inference** capability (research direction, not validated AGI breakthrough).

**Implications.** Mathematical and computational foundations for investigating hidden nested relationships and developing AI methods to discover and validate complementary structures. The broader Net Zero proposition remains the central open research hypothesis.

**Keywords:** Net Zero, complementarity, symmetry, asymmetry, mathematical decomposition, system equilibrium, hidden variables, computational inference, artificial intelligence.

## 3. Mathematical foundations (summary)

**Circle.** \(x^2+y^2=r^2\) → \(y_\pm=\pm\sqrt{r^2-x^2}\); \(A_++A_-=\pi r^2\); reflection \(R(x,y)=(x,-y)\), \(R\circ R=\mathrm{id}\).

**Sphere.** \(z_\pm=\pm\sqrt{r^2-x^2-y^2}\); \(V=2V_h=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\); \(R(x,y,z)=(x,y,-z)\).

**General decomposition.** For involution \(R\), \(f_\pm(x)=\frac{f(x)\pm f(Rx)}{2}\) ⇒ \(f=f_++f_-\); \(f_+\) invariant, \(f_-\) sign-reversing. For \(R(x)=-x\), even/odd.
