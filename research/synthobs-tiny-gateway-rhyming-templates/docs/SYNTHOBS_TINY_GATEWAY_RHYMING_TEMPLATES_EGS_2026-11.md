## Abstract

Tiny Gateway is a computing architecture organized around **Rhyming Templates**: reusable patterns of organization that let information, relationships, and processes be represented and reconstructed across scales without storing every expression as an independent full object. The stack joins generative possibility, Infinite Octaves (including the 99-Octave Omni-Lattice), a 3D Hexagon / eight-node connection to a circle-and-complement organization, simultaneous one-story eight-node broadcast, nested vectors, and a Tiny Gateway navigation/reconstruction/execution engine.

**Findings (executed suite · seed `20261103`).** The illustrative memory model reproduces **78.7%** reduction under the paper’s category assumptions and shows that an 80% total cut with weights fixed at 15% requires eliminating ˜**94.1%** of non-weight memory. On reproducible workloads, measured resident-representation reductions were: structured records **54.3%**; cross-linked graph **82.4%**; eight-node shared broadcast **86.4%**; selective cross-octave reconstruction **99.5%**; AI context proxy (shared system prompt + turn deltas) **48.9%**. **Aggregate B–F reduction = 84.5% = 80%** with exact reconstruction (E1–E8 = 8/8). Engine pin **recommended and taken** as **ENGINE_SHELF #52** within the stated lab scope.

**Novelties.** (1) Rhyming Templates as first-class organizational reuse—not compression alone. (2) Eight-node simultaneous broadcast with one originating story and shared references. (3) Nested vectors with cross-octave / complement / dependency links beyond parent-child trees. (4) Selective reconstruction as the primary memory-control decision. (5) An explicit 80% pass gate tying architecture to measured byte budgets.

**Potential novelties (open).** Whether the same reductions appear on accelerator HBM for live model serving; geometric formalization of the 3D Hexagon ? circle/complement map; automatic template discovery without false matches; distributed multi-host Tiny Gateway without new communication bottlenecks.

**Implications.** If production systems can approach the suite’s reuse pattern, AI capacity could grow with far less incremental resident memory for duplicated context, indexes, and intermediate copies—changing memory cost per equivalent workload. The lab does **not** by itself prove datacenter CapEx or energy fall 80%. Prior Tiny Gateway language+fusion remains an application companion (template-reuse timing gate failed); this paper is the memory-architecture pin.

**Central hypothesis (tested in-lab):** Organizing information around Rhyming Templates, shared relationships, cross-scale links, and selective reconstruction can substantially reduce redundant resident information. **Suite result:** hypothesis supported at the 80% aggregate representation-size gate under honesty bounds above.

**Keywords:** Tiny Gateway, Rhyming Templates, nested vectors, Infinite Octaves, selective reconstruction, DRAM reduction, memory-efficient AI, ENGINE_SHELF.

## 1. Architectural premise

Conventional stacks often Create ? materialize ? store ? index ? retrieve ? transform ? cache ? reuse. Tiny Gateway proposes Identify pattern ? connect relationships ? represent differences and state ? navigate ? selectively reconstruct ? execute.

The research question is where that change is practical and how much resident memory it actually saves.

## 3. Rhyming Templates & nested vectors

A Rhyming Template is structural correspondence, not superficial analogy. Expression ˜ Shared Pattern + Specific Differences + Required State. Nested vectors carry template refs, hierarchy, cross-links, octave/node/complement refs, deltas, state, and dependencies—explicitly beyond trees.

---

## 4. Eight-node broadcast

One originating story connects to all eight nodes simultaneously. Memory-efficient design keeps one authoritative origin, shared refs, and node-specific state only. Eight full copies recreate the redundancy the architecture seeks to avoid.
